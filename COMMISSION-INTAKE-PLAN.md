## Acceptance boundary to settle

The named consumer is the commissioning manager recovering the original
undertaking, including after losing the conversation. The population is new,
explicitly selected commission inputs accepted by C, not hypothetical future
results or an assumed legacy installed base.

The user selected the **delivery model**: C accepts the finite input
selection by producing a self-contained, verified commission bundle in an
explicit manager-chosen durable destination. The manager is the named custodian
of that delivered bundle. C's success means the copied undertaking is present
and independently readable there. It does not mean an auditor was launched,
read anything, agreed to the question or owes a return through C.

The user explicitly chose this over a Stewart-managed retained store, including
retaining partial bytes and diagnostics and supporting completion or an explicit
acknowledgment of incomplete intake. C does not undertake an independent retained
store, expiry service or result lifecycle. Returning a path or printing a digest
alone is not completed delivery.

Selected delivery-model obligations:

| Moment | Promise and responsible party | Required observation or continuation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before intake | Manager selects the original question, instructions, exact material, supplied names/view, guidance and declared grants/limits; manager has authority to supply the copies and chooses the destination/custodian. | Explicit finite selection; no inferred latest commission or recursive ambient repository capture. |
| Intake begins | Stewart records the requested identities and its planned destinations before material effects, with a reference known before the call. | Exclusive intent binding; a request key never silently repeats an uncertain operation. |
| Intake is incomplete | Stewart accounts for every planned/copied member and any failure. The attempt remains incomplete, not accepted. | Supported inspection and either completion within the original intent or an attributed disposition of the incomplete delivery; no private-state editing or deletion required. |
| Acceptance | Stewart has copied and verified every required original and made the detailed receipt and bytes recoverable independently of this process. | Completion record last, exclusive writes, verified persisted identities and an explicit bundle reference. Qualify platform durability; do not infer it from a successful write alone. |
| After delivery | Manager preserves the bundle at its chosen storage or through its own responsible preservation process. C provides independent readback and does not silently expire, remove or update the bundle. | Read-time verification, explicit identity selection, truthful missing/damaged/unsupported diagnostics, no fallback to original live sources. |
| Lost reply | The existing request/bundle reference locates the observed outcome. | Return the recorded acceptance if complete; expose an incomplete attempt otherwise. No fresh audit or newly inferred acceptance. |
| Unrecoverable intake | Stewart records what was and was not delivered and the attributed terminal disposition. | A failed response cannot erase copied bytes or an outstanding obligation. Disposition must specify custody of partial bytes and unmet promises. |

Terminal disposition for partial bytes follows the selected model: preserve them with the attempt
and its diagnostics in the same named custodian's destination, mark the requested
acceptance as not completed, and obtain explicit acknowledgment before recording
the intake undertaking as discharged. This grants no deletion. A failure to
record or verify that disposition remains unresolved. Retrying an investigative
question is outside C. This supported disposition must not become a family of audit retry facilities.

comes after the custody choice, and belongs to this commission only.

  material and supplied view, included guidance or transformations, and declared

  supplied by the manager, including a manager-prepared source snapshot. Any
  exclusions of links, special files, Git-history materialization or nested
  repositories are explicit useful limits, not silently omitted selections.
- Keep requested selection, observed accepted copies and unconfirmed actions
  distinct. Describe transformations explicitly; no claim that a declaration of
  access is actual enforcement or that selected bytes were read by an auditor.
- Use a stable, documented receipt presentation and explicit locator. Inspection
  must work after relocation and process/source loss, without executing recorded
  code or consulting a private originating installation. Discovery chooses
  nothing by recency, current directory or installation.
- Apply exclusive receipts, duplicate-key refusal, descriptor-safe regular-file
  reads, bound content identities, stable reason identifiers and actionable
  diagnostics. Preserve raw originals and failure records.
- Keep the commission namespace/version distinct from any future result format.
  Future work may reference this original undertaking without rewriting it.
  Do not create generic answer slots, a result envelope, a result registry or a
  legacy-result converter merely to anticipate that work.

Required meaningful comparisons, with an independent input witness retained
outside the operation and reviewed before any disposal:

1. Healthy finite commission: a new reader recovers the complete original
   question, selected bytes/view, guidance and declarations from the receipt.
2. Change selected input bytes: the accepted binding changes. Edit or remove the
   original sources after acceptance: the previously accepted bundle is invariant.
3. Two similar commissions: explicit references remain distinct; no latest-item
   guess is allowed. Moving a bundle and changing cwd does not change its identity.
4. Stop the originating intake process and use a separately started installed
   reader with only the bundle: ordinary recovery works without private state.
5. Interrupt intake before effects, during a copy, before completion and after
   completion before reply. Known intent exposes the actual outcome; completion
   or attributed disposition is supported without overwriting evidence.
6. Missing/corrupt selected material, invalid/duplicate-key records, existing
   destinations, changed sources, planted links and special files: diagnostics
   distinguish the condition, preserve partial evidence and do not follow unsafe
   paths or make unsupported acceptance claims. Include healthy firing controls
   and unaffected-member invariance for each measured property.

Document actual supported hosts and limits. The existing installed-wheel checker
continues to qualify Python when it is used; it cannot certify Rust. Build the
successor's installed-artifact checks alongside its code and report actual counts.
Tests of readback do not establish distributed availability, cryptographic
authentication of historical origin or host-wide sandbox enforcement.
