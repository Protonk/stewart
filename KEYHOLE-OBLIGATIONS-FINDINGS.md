# Obligations under a typed submission boundary

Document inquiry, 2026-10-25. **The candidate reductions are nonzero.** The
largest change is to authorize disposal of an exclusively owned working
environment for its commissioned lifetime, instead of justifying disposal by
capturing, reviewing and matching its changing contents. A narrow answer
boundary also removes several reasons for repeated collection, arbitrary
support export and private manager triage. These are prospective changes to
promises, not consequences already established by schema validation.

The user commissioned questions, independent agent review of the plans and
designs, then a reduction briefing and consideration of a Riker question.
CONTRACTS.md remains authoritative. This inquiry adopts no removal of an
obligation, changes no frozen Python bytes and establishes no harness boundary.

## Questions and assessment

The coordinator's questions separated seven issues: tree-specific custody;
the field of work during investigation; content judgment versus disposal
authority; review and failure duties; automatic retirement and early close;
new submission duties; and prior reductions/legacy scope. The exact brief is
retained in `.scratch/keyhole-obligations/QUESTIONS.md`.

Three independent 6.1 Sol agents at high effort examined custody, environment,
and counterexamples respectively. The integrated conclusion distinguishes
removing an obligation from narrowing its subject or moving it to a new
boundary. Removing custody of arbitrary scratch is a real reduction even
though custody of the submitted answer remains. No quantified code or effort
saving has been measured.

The second row is the most consequential reduction beyond the return channel.
Advance disposability alone does not remove today's content-identification
promise. To realize that saving, deliberately replace the subject of judgment:
the commissioned disposable lifetime and its boundaries, rather than a frozen
description of every descendant's bytes. Later readers would be able to verify
the disposal authority and outcome, but would no longer be promised an
enumeration of everything discarded. Filesystem traversal to remove entries
can remain necessary without becoming content capture or evidence hashing.

The final two rows are independent decisions. Neither follows automatically
from a narrow answer schema. Retaining a broad early-close salvage exception
would retain much of the machinery the strict boundary is meant to remove.

Mutable helper-runtime recovery omission was already a conditional proposal;
the keyhole strengthens its case but does not create that saving. The omitted
program executor, deferred general manager-resource service, removal of normal
live-readiness scans and narrowed blanket legacy compatibility were already
selected. Do not count them again. A simpler Git/workspace representation is
also a separate decision, not a consequence of a text-only return.

## The field of work remains a real responsibility

The answer channel does not prescribe a narrow investigative method. The
auditor may still need substantial selected material, history and useful
tools to answer a bounded question. The successor must still:

- Supply the commissioned inputs correctly, identify their source and limits,
  and prevent later manager changes from silently rewriting the question.
- Enforce granted access and effects through the admitted tool routes,
  protecting unselected and neighboring material. Shared immutable storage
  must expose only the selected view. A narrow return cannot undo an earlier
  unauthorized read or external write.
- Maintain ownership and lifetime separation for audit facilities, manager
  workspaces, external backing and other audits. Intended paths do not prove
  creation or disposal authority, particularly after failed preparation.
- Bind the actual run and stopping authority across human-run and agent-run
  launch commands. Submission admission does not prove that tools or
  descendants stopped. Safe retirement still needs control evidence.
- Account for interrupted preparation, submission, preservation and removal
  through supported operations, without another investigation or private-state
  repair. Keep holds and already accepted preservation undertakings effective.

These follow from [handoff](CONTRACTS.md#handoff-and-audit-owned-facilities),
[explicit grants](CONTRACTS.md#access-through-explicit-grants), the
[failure principles](CONTRACTS.md#principles), [close](CONTRACTS.md#close)
and [holds](CONTRACTS.md#holds-on-identified-custody-material).
A self-contained disposable environment could make its facility/dependency
account small. That is an arrangement to prove, not a property established
by calling a directory a container. A changed scratch file and an unrelated
directory substituted at the same path require different treatment.

## What moves to the submission boundary

The answer still needs a finite form, a commission/lifetime/run binding, a
durable identity distinct from its text digest, and a reader independent of
the original environment and writer. Original-question context must be
captured from the commissioned source, not reconstructed from an auditor's
edited copy or a commit identifier naming absent bytes. The exact finite
context remains to be selected; references do not recursively enlarge it.

The main unresolved undertaking is **when custody starts**. A setter might be
an ephemeral authoring convenience or a promise to save a draft. A received
final payload might be preserved before validation or admitted only after
validation under a deliberately revised raw-capture promise. Specify those
semantics rather than silently giving every call the strongest interpretation.
A schema-rejected submission can be successfully preserved: rejection,
transport truncation and failed custody writing are different facts.

An exclusive submission endpoint also reopens the decided
[helper-independent human handback](CONTRACTS.md#return-arrangements-and-the-human-auditor).
Provide an admitted manual equivalent or explicitly revise that accommodation.
A human author returning an answer is a different case from a person launching
the Claude auditor; supporting the latter does not establish the former.

Lost replies, racing final submissions, changed content under the same request
identity and interrupted writes still need supported outcomes. The service
must enforce bounds and interpret fields without executing or automatically
dereferencing text. Admission establishes the permitted representation;
scientific sufficiency, execution claims and useful conclusions retain their
attribution. A well-typed answer can still be useless or misleading.

Receiving an answer, delivering it, stopping execution, retiring the environment
and closing result custody remain distinct. The answer can survive cleanup
failure. Automatic environment retirement would be authorized prospectively
at commissioning, with durable valid submission and safe termination as
conditions; this revises the current rule that auditor completion grants no
disposal authority. It does not decide how long result custody lasts, require
indefinite retention, release holds or automatically complete custody close.
Manager-chosen retention of the result remains a separate matter.

## Decisions and evidence still needed

The coordinator recommends investigating the strict boundary without a general
tree-salvage exception. The smallest consequential decision set is:

1. Define the answer/context deliverable and the prospective disposable working
   lifetime, including limits on new holds or preservation promises that would
   contradict disposability. Existing undertakings cannot be overridden.
2. Choose whether to remove descendant content identities and duplicate-byte
   tests from retirement, while preserving facility identity and safe scope.
3. Define custody of setter drafts, attempted/rejected submissions and admitted
   finals; choose finality/correction, the early-close partial-work promise and
   whether helper-independent human handback remains supported.
4. Decide whether any compulsory managerial gap/follow-up response survives,
   separate from answer admission and automatic environment retirement.
5. Authorize the conditional retirement trigger, with result-custody lifetime,
   delivery, holds and interrupted operations separately accounted for.

The [answer-protocol inquiry](DEVELOPMENT-PLAN.md#answer-protocol-inquiry-before-the-successor-write-path)
now precedes the durable write path. Its representation comparisons must keep
recipients from consulting the richer source return until after they have
attempted the manager's task. Otherwise omitted support can remain invisibly
available. Fresh authoring should include unexpected late findings and
schema-valid but inadequate answers. Constrained experiment construction is
a particularly strong test: its existing recipe requires inspectable artifacts
and, for granted execution, trial and preservation evidence. A summary pointing
to a deleted specimen is not an adequate substitute. Any unsupported class must
be admitted as a capability limit, rather than changing the question recipe to
make the proposed form appear successful.

Integrated qualification must distinguish a schema rejection with preserved
payload, a failed custody write and a committed answer with a lost reply.
It must also distinguish an edited disposable file from substituted ownership,
and a stopped parent from surviving tool activity. The independent witness
stays outside disposal through review; rescue from it is not product retrieval.
No such trials were run in this inquiry.

## Riker and the transition

Riker's earlier [close-commitments reply](riker/CLOSE-COMMITMENTS-REPLY.txt)
already allows deliberate authorized loss and protects actual undertakings.
Re-asking whether valuable scratch must always survive would add little.
The [relay request](riker/KEYHOLE-COMMITMENTS-REQUEST.txt) asked which
submission-tool event creates an undertaking: successful setter use, receipt
of a final attempt, or durable final admission. The user has returned
[Riker's reply](riker/KEYHOLE-COMMITMENTS-REPLY.txt). It is advice, not a
decision or approval of either custody policy.

Both boundaries can be coherent. Setter success can promise only staging in
a disposable authoring draft, but its acknowledgement must say so; specific
language such as "saved" can contradict general disposable-draft terms.
Admission-only custody explicitly gives up recovery of unadmitted partial
answers. Attempt custody instead promises the **complete bounded answer
assembled for final submission**, including previously set fields, before
schema admission. Logging only the final call's arguments can lose the answer
while retaining evidence that a call occurred. Unsubmitted drafts remain
outside that narrower preservation promise.

The coordinator favors attempt custody as the first candidate to test. This
retains a useful partial-work promise at the deliberate handoff without general
tree salvage. Admission-only custody remains a coherent alternative. Neither
policy is selected. Under the favored candidate, distinguish these outcomes:

| Operation and outcome | Representation owed to the auditor and manager |
| --- | --- |
| Setter succeeds | Fields staged for continued authoring; no durable custody promised. |
| Complete attempted answer preserved; schema rejected | Identified attempted payload remains retrievable, but is not an admitted answer. |
| Promised attempt custody fails | Identify incomplete custody; do not describe the whole outcome as schema rejection or promise retrieval that failed. |
| Answer durably admitted; reply lost | Recover the same recorded answer; retry cannot downgrade it to an unadmitted attempt. |

This is a controlled widening of custody beyond admitted answer schemas.
Rejected payloads remain untrusted, bounded objects; their preservation gives
them no authority to select files, dereference paths or count as valid answers.
The protocol must define complete snapshot assembly and enforce its bounds
before promising custody. It must also specify attempt identity, repeated
attempt handling and custody lifetime: preserving attempts adds these duties,
and a later valid answer cannot silently erase earlier undertakings. Those
details are coordinator deductions to test, not additional claims from Riker.

The counterexample belongs in integrated qualification: stage substantial
findings, submit only a misspelled discriminator, then request graceless close.
Compare complete attempt capture with final-arguments-only logging; use a
corrected discriminator as a control and preserve an independent witness.
Verify that a later setter cannot change the captured attempt. Separately
test failed custody writing and lost admission acknowledgement. No admission
means the successful-completion retirement trigger has not fired; early close
still needs independent recorded authority and verified termination. Absence
of a preservation promise never supplies removal authority.

Existing Python audits, captured evidence, recoveries and unfinished operations
keep their actual obligations under the [bounded writer transition](CONTRACTS.md#writer-continuity-into-the-successor).
No old receipt becomes a typed submission or an advance disposal authorization.
Named legacy readers may still be useful; the old package format no longer
automatically determines the successor's result model.
