## Evidence limits and proposed discriminating comparisons

1. **Original identity/context:** distinguish two accepted commission receipts even with equal answer bytes; make C/live source unavailable after the new leg's context undertaking; compare a changed live original and unrelated damaged input. First potential loss is accepting only a locator/self-consistent replacement as the original binding. Retain only owed finite dependencies, not every mentioned input by implication.
2. **Operation boundaries:** cut before backend request publication, after element publication/before reply, and at adapter response delivery; return through the supported participant route using a pre-dispatch reference. Compare intentional equal-byte addition and unrelated element readback. Outcome unverifiable must stay uncertain. PROTOTYPE-RESULT 118–122 excludes the earliest binding cut from its qualified barriers; 30–35 development probes are not the product interface.
3. **Selection and arbitration:** pending add versus collect must account for whether membership committed; submit versus collect one winner; repeat the losing call and repeat copy after lost reply; a verifiable empty selection remains valid, unknown inventory does not become empty. Detailed pending-operation settlement is design work (INTERACTION-SIMPLIFICATIONS 163–168), bounded by attribution and uncertainty rules.
4. **Delivery and damage:** same fixed return, partial destination/full-disk interruption, completion-before-reply, known corrupt selected payload, missing original question, unknown manifest/provenance, unrelated unused preparation corruption. All must preserve useful verified material and distinct diagnostics; copying observed does not imply notification, human understanding, safety or close authority.
5. **Control/close invariance:** once collection fixes the return, later permitted attempts to edit must fail and another copy stays identical even if an external auditor process remains alive. Do not claim termination or test removal. A hold or retained superseded payload remains an obligation without becoming a selected return member. Unknowable-selection close remains deferred and unfinished.

Working assessment: no supported contradictory promise requiring a deferred control/removal capability. Concrete source-binding, supported re-entry, access and delivery acceptance are still mechanism/qualification work already assigned by the candidate. The candidate is not itself a dispatch-ready commission or runtime qualification. Unknown-selection disposal is genuinely open policy but not required for this no-removal leg.
