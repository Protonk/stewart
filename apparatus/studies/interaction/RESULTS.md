## Limits, pagination and long output

turn limits, a long checklist and log navigation. All closed. A ten-turn stop
returned a finished-looking manager-sealed account; an eight-turn stop returned
no entries. The pagination answer was correct using search and chosen tail reads,
so the offered next-page affordance was not exercised. Both checklist copies
spent a 16,000-token response in thinking and recorded nothing; the blinded plant
comparison was not reached. These are observations, not selected product changes.

Other observed friction included refused `read` calls with an unsupported `end`
key, a refused inexact quotation losing an entire revision, and an output cap
that includes thinking. The standalone answer omits operating history, so it
alone cannot distinguish all causes of a finished-looking or empty return.
