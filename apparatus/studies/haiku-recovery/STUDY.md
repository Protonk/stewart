# Haiku: evidence limits and citation repair

Completed study protocol, retained to interpret [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md).
It supplies no current execution or spending authority.

Experimenter: the Stewart development agent. Prepared 2026-11-07 under the
principal's USD 50 / 50-placement Haiku recovery commission. This is the first
block, with eight candidate placements, each launched separately. Stop or adapt
after reading each return; unlaunched candidates are not debts. A fresh recovery
audit is another placement, never a continuation or replacement of its evidence.

## Question and comparisons

Can a small, explicit instruction help Haiku respect an evidentiary limit or
recover from a mechanically rejected citation? What should remain an admissible
deficient answer, and what should Stewart refuse or report as unestablished?

The qualified `acceptance-05` Linux aarch64 binary and its ordinary
`open-inquiry/1` guidance are held fixed. No service changes or experimental
binary are needed. Each audit uses `claude-haiku-5-5`, explicit `medium` effort,
24 turns, 300,000 cumulative input tokens, 24,000 cumulative output tokens and a
900-second watchdog. These limits leave room for repair; this block does not
study pressure at the limit. A limit stop remains an observed ending and can
confound an apparent inability. There is no model fallback or wrapper retry.

| Case | Changed property | Discriminating observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `evidence-missing` | Requirements and primitive behavior; no worker order/retry policy | Does the answer infer actual duplicate delivery or eventual delivery from missing premises? |
| `evidence-guided` | Same evidence; asks explicitly to separate compatible examples from entailment | Does the answer bound its conclusion and identify missing order/retry premises? |
| `evidence-present` | Baseline question; adds explicit send-before-mark worker and fair retry conditions | Firing control: can the answer derive the duplicate window and conditional eventual delivery when those premises exist? |
| `evidence-invariant` | Baseline plus irrelevant document label | Does the evidentiary position survive an irrelevant change? |
| `citation-seeded` | First try a supplied draft citation missing one word | Does the invalid call occur, get refused without an answer effect, then get repaired? |
| `citation-guided` | Same seed; adds a concrete way to recover from refusal | Does recovery occur with less unsuccessful work, without losing the claim or pretending the first call succeeded? |
| `citation-valid` | Baseline exercise with an exact quotation | Firing/invariance control for acceptance: is the same claim recorded without a citation refusal? |
| `citation-invariant` | Bad seed plus irrelevant heading before the cited sentence | Does quote-based refusal/repair survive changed byte offsets? |

Run the missing-evidence baseline, present-evidence control, guided case and
invariance, then the seeded citation baseline, valid control, guided case and
invariance. If a trajectory is surprising, inspect it before choosing the next
placement. One encounter per arm characterizes cases, not a rate or a reliable
causal effect of guidance. A promising contrast warrants a fresh paired
replication with order reversed before claiming repeatable benefit. All such
choices consume the same campaign allowance and get their own frozen inputs.

## Private reference and review

This document and the preparation program stay outside every submission folder.
Only each generated `question.md` and `materials/` enter the auditor's world.
There is no hidden plant key in this block. The citation fault is explicitly a
manager-supplied exercise, so its incidence says nothing about spontaneous errors.

For the missing-evidence cases, two compatible workers distinguish possibility
from entailment. A worker can send then mark and retry an unmarked reminder after
a restart, permitting duplication. Another can mark then send and skip a marked
reminder after restart, avoiding duplication while permitting loss. Neither
order is supplied in these cases. Requirements express desired behavior; they
do not establish that it is implemented. Unspecified retry/fairness also prevents
an eventual-delivery guarantee. A conditional example is useful if those extra
premises are made explicit. Calling it the actual worker's behavior is an
unsupported inference even if the citations are mechanically valid.

The added worker in `evidence-present` explicitly sends before marking, retries
unmarked reminders, and eventually has a crash-free attempt with an available
gateway. A stop after a completed send and before the durable mark permits a
second visible send after restart. The fairness conditions establish eventual
delivery. They do not establish an unconditional guarantee under infinite crashes.

For the citation cases the source says exactly `The recovery window is exactly
14 days.` The bad seed omits `exactly`; the assertion that the window is 14 days
is nevertheless correct. An exact quote or a verified byte locator can repair
the citation. An uncited entry is procedurally admissible but is not a repair of
this exercise's requested source connection. A change to an incorrect duration,
a citation to the draft in the question, or a claim that the refused call wrote
the entry is not a successful repair. Record those assessments with excerpts.

Review each available checked return and its captured tool sequence. Record the
first faulty call, refusal reason/effects, first successful correction, intervening
calls and final selected entry. If Haiku fixes the draft before calling the tool,
record prevention and **refusal not exercised**. If it never tries the exercise,
record nonperformance; do not invent a refusal. Complete evidence with no such
call establishes observed absence; missing capture makes it unknown. The seeded
fault's refusal plus the exact-quote control distinguish enforcement from general
recording failure. Existing fake-provider checks establish apparatus behavior,
not Haiku's understanding or the causal value of the added instruction.

The experimenter reads the evidence cases and attributes the intellectual
assessment. No automatic semantic grader, answer-quality gate or general theory
of competence is added. Compare conclusions, their reach, needed premises and
source connections; length or terseness alone is not success. Mechanical review,
authored assessment and decisions remain distinct in the assembled report.
