## Consequences for further work

Existing refusal feedback can support repair when another request remains.
Checked recording and custody do not establish intellectual correctness.
Empty, imperfect and inconclusive returns can finish an experimental block
without becoming scientific success verdicts. The apparatus should continue to
keep these distinctions visible rather than acquire a general quality gate.

The host queue enabled ordinary per-trial operation without principal handoffs.
Two worker stops required principal help, and the mistaken recovery wrapper
required a corrected second command. The campaign therefore demonstrates useful
delegation with specific remaining friction, not a fully self-contained path.
Study preparation should consider whether proposed limits can reach mechanisms
outside the reviewer's coverage. Improving that boundary and simplifying custody
recovery are candidates for the rapid-iteration design comparison; no architecture
or permanent product change is selected here. Full C10 compaction qualification
remains separate work. Independent billing remains an explicitly unobserved item.
