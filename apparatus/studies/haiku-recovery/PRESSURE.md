# Repair when the turn allowance ends

Prepared by the Stewart experimenter on 2026-11-07, after reading first-block
trials 01–07. This follow-up uses the existing Haiku recovery commission. It
changes only packet turn limits; the already rehearsed templates, qualified
binary, medium effort, token allowances, route and watchdog stay fixed.

The seeded quotation was actually refused and then repaired in trial 05. Its
captured sequence used request 1 for the bad record and list, request 2 for read,
request 3 for search, request 4 for the corrected observation and account,
request 5 for prepare, and request 6 for submit. The valid quotation in trial 06
recorded immediately and submitted in five requests. These are observations,
not a promise that another trajectory will have the same prefix.

The question is what remains recoverable when a legitimate mechanical refusal
uses part of a very short allowance. Can a stopped, deficient return be handled
faithfully without calling manager closure an auditor submission or an expected
limit stop an operational failure?

| Selected condition | Comparison and prediction |
| --- | --- |
| `citation-seeded`, two turns | Likely no recorded entry: bad record refused, then source read before the limit. Inspect actual effects; an empty answer is a legitimate observed outcome. |
| `citation-valid`, two turns | Same allowance and baseline exercise, exact quote. A successfully recorded first entry should survive even if submission is not reached. This is the recording control. |
| `citation-seeded`, four turns | More allowance may permit a repaired entry/account while still stopping before auditor submission. Compare with the already observed complete six-request trajectory under 24 turns. |

The measured properties are the recorded ending and cause, request count,
attempted/refused/accepted operations, surviving entries, actual seal actor,
verified delivery and closure. Read the returned text separately and attribute
its assessment. Do not assume a lower cap truncates an otherwise identical
trajectory: notices and changed allowances can affect behavior from the start.
If the model submits within a short allowance or records earlier than predicted,
keep that result and revise the account rather than forcing an empty return.

After each limit stop, inspect the original checked export. Under the existing
delegation, request ordinary manager close, inspect the delivered selection,
confirm that exact delivery and collect a new immutable snapshot. Keep the early
open-custody snapshot and the later closed one. Never resume the old placement,
silently substitute a fresh run, surrender loss or erase a failed operation.

A larger-allowance fresh audit can demonstrate practical recovery without
repairing the original audit's absent submission. This block relies first on the
existing successful larger-allowance control; commission another only if a new
comparison is needed. Three new placements are candidates, not a required quota.
Their costs join the same USD 50 / 50-placement campaign accounting. No service
change, new credit machinery or new scientific quality gate is proposed.
