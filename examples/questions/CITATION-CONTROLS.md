# Small citation controls for the coordinator

Paper controls, not executed product tests. Use the open ordinary packet.
The exact byte range below selects one requirement sentence without its newline.

Valid structured citation:

```json
{
  "material": "m1",
  "start": 50,
  "end": 119,
  "quote": "R1. A reminder ID should cause at most one recipient-visible message."
}
```

For a mismatching-quote control, replace `at most one` in the quote with `exactly
one` while leaving the range unchanged. The proposed mutation must refuse and
retain the attempted call without adding an answer element.

For the valid-but-irrelevant control, attach the valid citation above to the
claim `The service encrypts every stored reminder.` The reference is mechanically
valid and the claim remains the auditor's; Stewart must not declare it supported
or reject it on an inferred support judgment. A reader may contest it.

An empty selection can prepare and submit; the rendering must say only that no
answer entries were recorded. Two contradictory accounts can both be recorded
and rendered in order without choosing one or inventing a reconciliation.
