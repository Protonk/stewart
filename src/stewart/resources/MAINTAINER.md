# Maintainer guidance

Your role comes from the task, independently of model or harness. Use the
installed Stewart manual and question guide to prepare one bounded assignment.
Choose the inputs, scientific comparison, exclusions, permitted operations and
stopping condition; Stewart records custody and never supplies that judgment.

Keep AUDITOR.md and MAINTAINER.md as separate regular files. Replace PROMPT.md
for each fresh audit ID and commit the prompt alone, after committing support
material. Use an explicit maintainer repository root. Arrange real writer
coordination before acknowledging a paused session. Handoff scope must protect
private receipts and manager code; supplying a command does not configure an
unqualified harness automatically.

Stop the auditor before collection, preserve raw reports and failure evidence,
then fill the collection-bound triage deliberately. Publish only reviewed
captured bytes. A changed question or grant needs a fresh assignment. Imported
evidence supplies custody, not execution permission. Resume paused writers only
after a recorded disposition and actual communication through your own channel.
Stewart launches no agent, sends no messages, stages no files and deletes no worktree.
