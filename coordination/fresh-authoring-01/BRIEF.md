## What this would establish

The previous [finite-return study](../return-boundary-01/RESULT.md) curated
existing reports and notes after investigation. This trial asks whether an
auditor can select the support that must cross the boundary while doing new
construction work. The recipient must inspect the construction without access
to the author's scratch. Manager-owned pinned subject source remains available.

The undertaking is to return a bounded, inspectable construction with its basis
and limitations. It is not a promise of a working reproduction environment,
an observed counterexample, a complete search, or recovery of every draft.
No existing custody or partial-work undertaking is relinquished by this study.

## The auditor's question

Can you construct a small comparison that distinguishes these explanations:

- A plausible number of comparison rows, ordinary operand agreement and a
  firing control suffice to establish reviewed-measurement coverage.
- Coverage depends on each admitted case supplying exactly its independently
  reviewed measurement IDs, once each, with the necessary event sequence.

Use the frozen Python ledger at the pinned revision as the subject. State what
its actual verification would be predicted to detect or miss for your chosen
comparison. A predicted refusal can answer the question; discovering a bypass
is not required. If the distinction cannot be constructed under the grant,
identify the premise or different question needed. No supported finding,
needs-evidence, reframe and qualified partial work are legitimate returns.

**Contract and observables.** Examine parse_rows, summarize with explicit
review cases, and prerequisites. Interpret parse problems separately from
prerequisites; both belong in the proposed observation. Use populations,
membership anomalies and operand-derived disagreements as needed. The subject
is this pure verification route, not the wrapper's custody, exit code, actual
release authenticity, or the truth of the child-supplied operands.

**Variation.** Author a finite review-case fixture and JSONL specimens. Freeze
that review across the central pair. It is an authored hypothetical input,
not an actual released review or managerial admission. Its cases must meet the
pinned review validator's case constraints; control JSON cannot contain repeated
keys. Keep raw-ledger decoding distinct. Change ordinary measurement membership
while keeping comparison-row count, ordinary operands, control rows and legal
event scaffolding fixed wherever possible. Explain any necessary co-variation.
Include an operand-sensitive firing control and a permitted invariance, such
as changed notes or object-key order. Choose the cases independently; supplied
tests are examples, not an answer key. An invalid construction or competing
explanation is a result to expose, not a reason to claim the intended contrast.

**Protected state.** Target code, supplied tests, review fixture across the
paired variants, and any proposed checker/reference remain identified and
unchanged where their stability is part of the comparison. Distinguish the
author's promised preservation, coordinator-observed source hashes and any
future execution witness. Before/after hashes alone cannot prove continuous
preservation. Nothing executed or measured is claimed in this pilot.

**Grant.** Construction only: read the issued finite source packet, write
specimens and explanation in the assigned private attempt directory, and submit
selected bytes through the study interface. No project imports, trials, tests,
installation, Git mutation, network research or existing custody operations.
The script/command proposed for later execution may be written and inspected
but must not run. An exact command and its environmental prerequisites are

Proposed development commands name an explicit interpreter with isolated startup

## Candidate return boundary

Use a small common envelope: question/source identity, attributed response kind
and completion claim, freely written answer, and an explicit finite selection
of support objects. Claims of completion remain the author's statements. The
interface must permit qualifications, disagreement, missing premises and
unexpected findings without selecting a preferred outcome.

For this question, a completed construction ordinarily needs these contents;
their arrangement is the author's choice:

| Content | Inspection it enables |
| --- | --- |
| Exact review fixture and JSONL specimen bytes | Inspect membership, event order, escaping and operands without reconstructing them from prose. |
| Hypotheses, predicted observations and invariance | Distinguish the intended comparison from row counts, control firing or another changed property. |
| Proposed checker or precise invocation, with assumptions | Assess whether a later bounded execution would answer the question and what it would require. |
| Qualifications and unresolved work | Preserve the difference between authored artifacts, static predictions and observations; explain incomplete construction when relevant. |

A support object has an identifier, a stated role, content bytes and encoding.
The snapshot records its size and digest. Logical names do not select filesystem
paths; submission carries bytes, with no recursive directory collection or
automatic dereferencing of references. Text and source files suffice for this
first case. Exact escaped text must not be replaced by a visually similar
rendering. This allowance does not establish support for every artifact type.

The coordinator supplies the question and pinned subject source independently
to recipients. The author selects its own reasoning, fixtures and other created
support. A reference to manager-owned subject material is valid only when that
material is in the issued recipient grant. A reference into author scratch does
not make those bytes part of the return.

Recommend a generous provisional study bound of 1 MiB total submitted bytes and
32 support objects, with no prose-length target. These are engineering bounds
for one pilot, not empirically justified product limits. The prototype must
report exceeded bounds without truncation; attempted content and the author's
account of what would be lost stay with the independent study witness. A cap
failure is evidence about the candidate boundary, not an inadequate audit.

An observer preserves drafts and attempted submissions separately for review.
That witness is unavailable to initial recipients. It supplies no product
retrieval or custody assurance and cannot rescue the primary result silently.
No actual working environment is removed.

## Smallest proposed dispatch

One fresh author constructs through this common interface. Save a provisional
draft and then permit a final check within the same source/variation grant:
does the selected exact specimen express the distinctions the prose depends
on? Record changes and their reasons before freezing the final submission.
This prompted check is not itself an unexpected finding. If none arises, actual
late-finding coverage remains open; a later comparison must exercise it.

After freezing, three fresh recipients receive the same original commission,
pinned subject source and one return variant each:

1. Complete author-selected return.
2. One consequential created support object omitted, with its reference and
   the claim it supports retained. Record the altered selection honestly;
   do not fake byte verification. Freeze this omission before recipient work.
3. Complete return with support-object presentation order changed and all
   bytes and identifier associations preserved.

Each recipient identifies the exact proposed experiment, what it would
distinguish, what remains predicted, and whether to commission execution,
request named material, reframe, or stop. They do not execute it. Freeze the
initial memo before revealing the omitted support; show the same object to
full-content controls and record the change in grounds separately from the
recommendation. If no suitable independently meaningful omission exists, record
that limitation rather than manufacture a firing control.

The full/omitted pair tests a changed content property; reordering is the
intended invariance control. The omission must actually obstruct the named
inspection and restoration must repair it before claiming sensitivity. A
non-firing omission, changed control response, or redundant support limits the
conclusion. One author and three recipients cannot establish general adequacy,
causal interface effects or equivalence. Comparing question-specific fields
requires repeated fresh authors with comparable grants in a later commission.

An independent reviewer inspects the submitted selection, witness and recipient
memos. At most four agents including the coordinator are active together; no
nested delegation is needed. Suggested future budget: 60–90 minutes including
prototype checks, construction, recipient passes and integration; revise after
the prototype is concrete. The prototype worker has implemented and checked the
helper; [progress](PROGRESS.md) records its review and remaining preparation.
No author or recipient has been dispatched.

## Assessment and stopping conditions

The primary failure is that inspecting the submitted construction requires
recovering author-created material left outside it. Freeze that observation
before any witness reveal. Inspecting an author's reasoning, independently
checking subject code, and running the proposed experiment are separate tasks.
Requests for manager-owned source or a new execution are not automatically
return failures. Classify each requested dependency and the purpose it serves.

Also record lost qualifications or attribution, incorrect or unsupported
predictions, reconstruction effort, cap pressure, reliance on free text, and
whether the interface suppresses an unexpected result. Attribute substantive
disagreements; agreement with historical findings or the coordinator's expected
answer is not success. A legible but invalid experiment is different from an
opaque return, and both differ from successful durable delivery.

Before author/recipient dispatch, confirm a suitable machine window for the
authorized pilot and review the checked replaceable prototype outside frozen
Python; freeze exact recipient packets,
source copies, grants, identities, output paths and witness timing; and save
bounded worker briefs. Prototype checks must cover exact bytes, changed content
and order invariance, plus visible size rejection without silent data loss.
The snapshot is a study observation, not a chosen product custody-start event.
Admission-only versus complete-attempt custody, corrections, early close,
human handback, stopping, delivery and disposal remain separately undecided or
unqualified. This pilot does not complete W01 or authorize withdrawing duties.
