## Integration judgment

Most of the old handoff's bulk already has a suitable owner. Preserve a short
current account pointing to the program and dispatch evidence, transfer the
coordinator-specific rules from AGENTS, and carry the useful lessons with their
status. The transformation does not need another product synopsis or another
full evidence inventory. The details most likely to be lost or promoted in error
are personal rerunning of tests, the Git-guide's requested form, retention until
Rust acceptance, and the difference between a current commission and a proposed
next wave.

The following distinctions matter:

- The real installed-wheel checker is an established contributor requirement
  (A:163–174; DEVELOPMENT.md, “Running the suite”). Personally rerunning every
  worker's tests is a historical coordinator recommendation (H:253–255), with
  a concrete failure behind it. The old handoff does not establish a separate
  user instruction requiring duplicate runs. Preserve the lesson as attributed
  advice to verify the real checker and saved evidence, not a new mandatory
  personal rerun gate. The `setup`-helper failure and failed coordinator rename
  already survive in CUSTODIAL-CORE-FINDINGS.md:1171–1189 and P:1261–1273.
- The Git document's three-part shape is a recorded user request, not merely
  incidental layout. `a792774` introduced the request, including handwritten
  narrative/guide and a mechanically source-generated table; P:830–837 also
  records it. Its proper destination is W18/publication guidance. Keep its
  exact form and commands there; do not turn it into the format for all docs.
- General run-artifact disposal is owned by DEVELOPMENT.md:169–221 and
  A:171–174. H:344–346 is a program-specific retention account, not a new
  universal rule. `be5e6ba` tied stage-1/2 findings and run-record retention to
  Rust acceptance; `85b957b` carried that account into stage 4. P:341–342
  expressly covers status findings; P:645–647 and older findings give
  purpose-served retirement wording for other reports. Preserve the current
  baseline comparison evidence pending its acceptance/replacement disposition
  in the program account; do not resolve the wording tension by deleting any
  material or creating a permanent all-findings retention order here.
- Consultation is optional. Riker's reasoning is advice even when the user
  relays it. The two reliance questions were adopted by the coordinator inside
  a specific user-authorized status commission (P:422–454); the underlying
  boundary over purpose, scope and protected obligations is standing user
  direction (A:58–84). Keep the questions as a useful reasoning aid, with that
  source, rather than presenting every phrasing change as needing approval.
- Some A paragraphs mix coordinator duties with rules for every worker and
  product boundaries. Transfer by meaning, not whole paragraph. In particular,
  the Claude launch/resume permission restriction applies to any development
  agent, not only the coordinator. It must remain reachable as a contributor
  rule or be unambiguously imposed on every worker; a coordinator-only route
  must not make it disappear for workers. Product/frozen-baseline rules remain
  root guidance and CONTRACTS-owned, not coordinator standing orders.

## Compact standing-order set supported by existing authority

1. Own the coherent development result inside the commission: investigate scope
   and protected obligations, choose meaningful assignments and exclusive write
   ownership, integrate and verify; reserve purpose/scope/obligation changes to
   the user. Preparing a plan is not execution permission.
2. Keep coordination recoverable continuously. The maintained account/index
   records current commission, pointers, observed dispatch state and next action;
   briefs and worker checkpoints exist before and during work. The optional thin
   turnover is only a fallible end-of-session hint and may be blank.
3. On intake/interruption, inspect actual files, retained logs and harness state.
   Do not infer liveness or effects from silence, old timestamps or a missing
   reply. Separate writers until a prior one is confirmed stopped; record what
   verified saved work a new attempt adopts.
4. Use the harness and useful concurrency; obey the soft whole-fleet preference
   and lower harness limits. No fixed staffing/provider split, no capacity
   evasion, and no implicit Claude permission.
5. Preserve source/attempt identities, save outputs before chat returns, and
   distinguish returned from verified/integrated. Keep unresolved work and
   evidence disposition explicit. Existing evidence policy controls disposal.
6. Own Git logistics and coherent verified commits; communicate semantic outcomes,
   evidence and consequential questions rather than asking the user to manage
   staffing or Git.
7. Maintain a useful, revisable account without user supervision; preserve
   costly context with provenance/status, retire duplication after giving it an
   adequate owner, and keep program details out of standing orders.
8. Assess advice independently, preserving provenance. Consultation is optional;
   relayed advice is not automatically the user's instruction. Maintain the
   development/product boundary.

These summarize A and the accepted commission. They deliberately do not add a
new required review count, test count, document shape, recurring approval stage,
personal rerun requirement or evidence-retention horizon.
