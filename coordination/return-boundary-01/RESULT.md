## Dependency disposition

The [fourteen-row map](dependencies/attempt-01/FINDINGS.md) separates auditor-created
support, manager-owned subject/question material, mutable audit environment,
custody/delivery and control/holds. Return-only access is a diagnostic restriction,
not a proposed prohibition on managers using their own pinned subject source.
Needing such a source is different from needing discarded auditor scratch.

| Purpose/connection | Evidence and disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Browse the original report and selected notes to inspect what the auditor said | All original report/note bytes are in the full candidate. Readers inspect central arguments, retain qualifications and contest wording. Content replacement is supported for these selected tasks; product capture/retrieval remains unqualified. |
| Recover an auditor's supplied support argument | Four bounded omissions leave central decisions stable but remove an explicit basis restored later. Preserve identified support/attribution or explicitly change the undertaking; a stable conclusion is insufficient reason to discard it. |
| Independently check the subject implementation | Newly selected pinned excerpts enable direct checks and qualifications. This can be a manager-owned subject connection, separate from auditor scratch. Missing helpers remain named requests; no self-contained universal verification promise is inferred. |
| Author a finite return under a prospective restriction | Not tested. Both candidates retain every original report/note byte and grow in size. Post-hoc curation says nothing about authoring pressure or selecting support before a workspace ends. |
| Actually stop, capture, deliver, recover or retire | Not tested or authorized for reduction. A recipient reading a copied file is not an ordinary Stewart retrieval, a stop witness or disposal authority. |

Mandatory manager replies and early-close partial-work salvage remain separate
policy choices. So do custody start, rejected/partial attempts, correction/finality,
human handback and the concrete control/delivery route. Holds and unfinished
preservation duties survive any later environment retirement until their supported
disposition. The removed executor and deferred general manager-resource service
are not counted again as new savings.

The next discriminating content work should be **prospective fresh authoring**:
can an auditor select and express the required support under a finite undertaking,
including uncertainty and late findings, without a coordinator rescuing it from
the whole workspace afterward? That is a recommendation for the next commission,
not dispatched work. Construction/receipt-sufficiency and actual needs-evidence,
reframe or partial-return cases remain untested. The additional ledger helpers,
A1 retry/finish/fixture source, escaped-surrogate specimen, concrete attempt/capture
records and fresh runtime comparisons requested here are explicitly unrun; this
study does not expand automatically to satisfy them. Actual operating-route and
custody/failure qualification must follow their own bounded commission and policy
decisions before any protected undertaking is withdrawn.
