# Assessment before recipient responses

This inquiry tests a proposed boundary: a recipient receives an identified finite
return and the original question, without continuing access to the audit's working
environment or source. It does not test intellectual utility, impose a desired
answer, or confer completeness on the auditor's statements. Preserve substantive
judgments, disagreement, no finding, uncertainty and reframing as outcomes.

## Discriminating observations

For each memo record the claim the recipient can make, its scope and attribution;
which supporting passage carries each consequential step; what remains a prediction,
an auditor report, a current observation or an unresolved assertion; and the
smallest missing material or new inquiry required for the recipient's next step.
Distinguish understanding the auditor's stated basis from independently verifying
it. A request for a new execution is not automatically a defective return.

The bounded omission is intended to expose loss of one support argument while
preserving its conclusion, surrounding qualifications and other evidence. Compare
explicit support/gap handling, exact use of the omitted passage after restoration,
and residual corroboration elsewhere. Do not infer private detection from silence
or a causal effect merely from different words. An omission is only a proposed firing control: retained passages may supply
redundant reasoning, and the recipient task may not require that exact step. A
non-firing control prevents a clean claim of sensitivity or dispensability; it is
a limitation requiring explanation, not evidence that omitted material is useless.

A content-identical reordering, if admitted, tests sensitivity to presentation while
holding original statements fixed. It is an intended invariance comparison, not a
guarantee of stable answers. Fresh repeated recipients help expose variation;
they do not establish a population effect. No response is graded by historical
manager agreement. Historical manager reviews are not needed for this wave.

## Staged restoration

Freeze each initial memo and read inventory before any additional grant. First
restore the single omitted support unit, showing the same passage to full-content
controls, then freeze a separate delta memo. Ask what changes, why, and what still
requires more material. Later grant selected historical source only if it addresses
a named uncertainty; record exact selection and a separate response. The full
original report and notes remain available to review but are not an automatic
extra reveal that could confound the bounded omission. Source availability
without actual use cannot be counted as evidence of independence or verification.
If a phase is not run, mark it unrun. Never revise the initial response in light
of a reveal. Preserve original evidence outside the omission population.

## Limits and authority

Return-only access is a diagnostic restriction, not a proposed rule that managers
lose their own subject source. Classify a requested dependency as retained return,
manager-owned pinned subject material, a finite new specimen, a new investigation,
or the auditor's private working environment. Needing the manager's own source
does not by itself defeat ending the audit-environment connection. A broader
self-contained verification promise would need explicit selection and evidence.

These are retrospective conversions of two static historical returns, not fresh
schema-constrained authoring. Finite returns may be long; organization alone is
not size reduction. A successful content boundary cannot establish setter/finality
semantics, attempts custody, stopping, enforcement, safe removal, held material,
recovery, durable delivery or helper-independent access. Compulsory manager replies
and early-close salvage are separate policy decisions. Distinguish replacing a
purpose from relinquishing it, and either from deferring proof.

Read restrictions are instructions, not an OS sandbox. Capture agent identities,
actual source reads and digests, supplied context, packet identities, failures,
output truncation, elapsed observations and unrun stages. No historical execution,
Claude, runtime edits, Git writes, custody operations or pruning. Actual final
checks and response counts will be reported; no pytest nodes are selected.
