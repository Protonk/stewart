# A3 finite return design

This is an editorial specimen for instrument discrimination, not a product schema or an assessment of the historical audit's correctness. The recipient has the same question and recipe in every condition and initially has no historical source repository. The full packet carries every byte of the original report and both notes, divided into 43 attributed blocks and arranged around the recipient's decision. Nothing substantive was compressed, corrected, inferred or converted to a verdict. No historical code ran.

## Connection, purpose and possible replacement

The current connection under examination is the recipient's ability to browse the auditor's environment or original repository after receiving a report. That connection serves several distinct purposes:

| Purpose | Finite replacement proposed here | Limit that remains |
|---|---|---|
| Recover the auditor's stated answer, qualifications and unresolved matters | Full report and two notes; historical declaration retained; thematic arrangement adds no result | Does not establish that the author could have produced this packet under a restricted authoring interface |
| Examine the reasoning actually supplied for the empty-`unmet` membership inference | P1–P6, algorithm account, P4 proof sketch, explicit review and 18-row specimen, mutation arms and rival predictions | Auditor assertions and proof sketches remain attributed; source agreement requires a distinct check |
| Identify what a reported zero means and what it excludes | Acceptance rule, review membership, aggregate-control limit, raw decoding and equality qualifications, process/custody separation, closing wording | No actual attempt or retained measurement ledger was supplied to this historical static audit |
| Check whether a claimed source behavior agrees with source | A separately proposed bounded source-excerpt restoration, with captured source identities | Reading source is a new independent check of an old claim; it is not recovery of omitted auditor reasoning |
| Obtain an observed contrast or empirical zero | No replacement is present in this packet | Execution requires another bounded commission; packets cannot manufacture observations from predictions |
| Verify original capture, actual stopping or delivery | Source identity metadata only | No enforcement, stop acknowledgement, delivery, lost-reply handling or complete custody chain is demonstrated |

The proposed ended connection is therefore narrow: original-environment navigation need not be retained merely to inspect the historical auditor's complete stated basis. It remains an open empirical question whether a recipient can use this finite undertaking without important accidental dependence on layout or external context. It is not a proposal to extinguish the recipient's ability to commission a new inquiry.

## What the finite return carries

The six editorial parts arrange (1) attributed answer and permitted wording, (2) evidence class and scope, (3) mechanism and completeness argument, (4) specimens and contrasts, (5) fixture and decoding qualifications, and (6) unresolved work and stopping account. Source attribution precedes every block. All historic assertions remain verbatim, including qualifications, potentially awkward wording and claims the recipient might dispute. Headings explicitly marked “Editorial” and the introductory paragraphs are the only new explanatory prose inside the finite packets.

The following cannot be removed merely as report clutter without changing this undertaking:

- “Static prediction”, “asserted but not run”, and “no retained observation” limits; the eight-input verification is identity at read time, not proof of execution or coverage.
- The conditional link between empty `unmet`, complete reviewed membership and recomputed counts, including the release review's role, population assumptions and version/capture assumptions.
- The operand-sensitive firing control, independent membership substitution, the true-zero comparison, and the event-order invariance with its precedence boundary. A firing control alone cannot stand in for the selected population.
- K-swap's aggregate-control limitation and the distinction between existing retained-row inspection and running a new measurement.
- Raw JSON last-key behavior versus control decoding, numeric and string qualifications, standalone versus released-review authority, and the separate meanings of measurement prerequisites and custody/process status.
- The exact authored rows and predicted output arms. A summary of their conclusions would deprive a recipient of the means to inspect the proposed distinctions.
- No evidence request, optional observation follow-up, remaining maintainer judgments, and the stopping/failure/deviation account. “Answer” must not become a demand that recipients agree.

This design pressures a return to carry reasons, counterfactual specimens, populations, conditions, evidence class, provenance and open matters. It does not select a production field vocabulary. A free-text annex can carry irreducible support; the 56,766 original bytes are not treated as gratuitous merely because a shorter conclusion is possible. The full finite packet is larger than the baseline because it adds explicit block attribution and organization. Repetition is retained to avoid a paraphrase/selection confound.

## Matched support omission

The diagnostic packet removes exactly one 700-byte support argument from notes 01, P4: the two-direction proof sketch relating accepted compare rows to P(R), including its closing sequence-problem sentence. The P4 proposition, per-case sequencing premises, planned-refusal qualification, consequence, P3's anomaly mapping and every other claim remain unchanged. Original source bytes 8029–8729 (zero-based, half-open) are retained separately in `restorations/p4-proof.txt`; its SHA256 is `eb0dbf99e269d7ea1e366b2b57ec12d8e30ada336d589530be76dfc40e93502b`.

Why this omission could matter: the central answer uses an empty `unmet` list as evidence of complete uniquely identified membership. The deleted span explicitly connects row-to-set and set-to-row obligations to anomaly categories. A reader who has the conclusion but cannot inspect this chain may need the missing argument before deciding what the answer warrants.

Why a null effect is ambiguous: the packet still includes the detailed algorithm and state-machine account, from which a capable recipient may reconstruct the reasoning. The omission tests one supplied argument, not the absence of all support for the claim. It does not establish that similar arguments are generally dispensable or that authoring can be constrained to conclusions.

There is no cut marker or renumbering. The report's existing “proof sketch … notes 01, P3–P4” reference remains. This can create a legitimate semantic cue that the reasoning is thinner; it is not a newly inserted treatment label. P4 and its consequence still read continuously. All treatment metadata and this explanation stay outside recipient packets.

## Content-identical order control

The fourth packet swaps only the adjacent complete attributed units N1-02 (“Route traced”) and N1-03 (“summarize, step by step”). They are support accounts within the same editorial part. Every source block, attribution and other editorial byte is unchanged. All report answer, claim, qualification, declaration and stopping blocks stay at their original offsets. Inverting the swap reproduces the full packet exactly. The order map permits this to be reviewed without treating a new hash as a changed claim.

This control probes dependence on route-before-detail ordering. It does not test every order, parsing model or rendering condition. A later recipient comparison should not read packet filenames, design documents, source maps or check outputs that disclose the treatment.

## Restoration and rival explanations

First let the recipient state the disputed inference and the smallest missing material, if any. A request to see the P4 argument can be answered with the exact 700-byte span and its location. A change after that restoration concerns access to the auditor's stated basis. It need not agree with the auditor's conclusion to count as useful inspection.

A request to verify that `summarize` actually implements the claimed mapping has a different purpose. Proposed source excerpts carry selected captured code/manual bytes separately and explicitly as new editor-supplied material, never as if the historical auditor returned them. They let a recipient begin an independent source check. Further helper or test bodies may still be needed for a narrower question; all dependencies are not certified merely by naming their files.

A request to observe the authored contrast is a new investigation. None of the packets, omitted-support restoration or source snippets supplies execution evidence. A source check or experiment that changes a recipient's judgment must be reported separately from restoration of missing returned support.

The original files remain an independent witness outside every candidate and omission. Nothing was deleted. Static checks verify byte preservation and the single-change controls; they do not verify intellectual adequacy, the original algorithm, historical execution or real access enforcement.

## Read scope and methodological limits

`READS.jsonl` records source paths, whole-file SHA256, bytes, scope and UTC timestamps. Required intake included the global `inputs-01.json`; its metadata contains other-case paths and held-out manager-review identities. The initial combined display was truncated, but that file was read in full by the shell and later hashed. No held-out review content, other worker result, earlier pilot response, coordinator account or plan outcome was opened. This metadata exposure must not be described as complete blindness to the existence/identity of held-out material.

The question guide was read in full during present packet design; only the exact Instrument discrimination section is supplied as common recipient material. Historical original grant restrictions are preserved as evidence of the historical task, not silently claimed as the present operator's observed behavior. Root AGENTS and standing orders were read as required; no general coordinator intake was performed because the bounded worker brief excluded those accounts.

Source-excerpt reading is selected, static and declared in the read log. No tests, project imports, network actions, Git mutations, deletions, nested delegation or manager-review reads occurred. This is an offline content construction study; recipient use, enforced restricted authoring/access, durable delivery and custody remain untested.

## Residual corroboration after the P4 cut

The missing proof is not informationally isolated. The following remain in both full and withheld packets; block IDs are review instrumentation and do not need to be exposed to recipients.

| R-05, complete gate finding | Says all 15 anomaly lists map to prerequisites and gives a concrete omission with unchanged summary | Corroborates a gate premise and one failure contrast, not universal completeness alone |
| N1-03, summarize step by step | Describes declaration, reach, admission, compare, review conflict, missing/duplicate/unexpected handling and counted sets | Together with P3 and the assumptions, a reader may reconstruct essentially the deleted reasoning |

| N1-07, P3 | Complete anomaly-to-unmet mapping and implication that empty unmet means no anomaly | Supplies the key exclusion premise used by both proof directions |
| N1-08, surviving P4 | Bijection proposition, ordered event premises, planned-refusal qualification and consequence | The asserted chain is visible even though its explicit anomaly-by-anomaly proof sketch is absent |
| R-11 and N2-02/N2-03/N2-05 | Exact baseline rows, membership-edit predictions and rival explanations | Particular contrasts support discrimination but do not prove the universal inference |

Thus the contrast can distinguish access to this explicitly supplied support passage, including whether it changes what a recipient can inspect or asks to restore. It cannot cleanly distinguish “support exists” from “no support exists”, and a non-firing result cannot establish uselessness. The removal was not widened to force a response. Even a change after restoration might reflect salience or reading order; the separate order control probes one such presentation sensitivity but does not remove all rivals.
