# A3 representation pilot

Completed and independently reviewed 2026-10-26. Recipient work finished by
19:12 UTC; integration review found no substantive blocker.
This is one diagnostic pilot with four fresh recipients and two staged reveals
per recipient. No Claude call, fresh product audit, runtime implementation or
durable answer-schema decision. The [commission](COMMISSION.md) and
[pre-response assessment](ASSESSMENT.md) define the scope.

## Result

The omission arm exposed a distinction that a conclusion-agreement measure
would have missed: its recipient reached a similar conditional account by
reconstructing missing reasoning from the shared source. After the original
support was revealed, the recipient could attribute that reasoning to the
historical auditor instead. A recoverable argument is not the same observation
as an argument preserved in the answer payload.

Original, common and reordered recipients reported that the main argument was
directly available; they also inspected source to check it. The reordered
recipient expressed a similar account, with no reported missing central support.
This is no causal finding that ordering is harmless or that the forms are
equivalent. All recipients used source search, and one recipient per arm cannot
separate presentation effects from recipient variation.

All four first memos kept the historical audit's static reasoning distinct from
an observed measurement. That is an observed preservation of an evidence-class
distinction in this setting, not a grade for agreeing on the substantive answer.
None of the first memos directly endorsed the original recorder-origin claim;
that silence does not establish private detection or rejection of the claim.
The later historical manager review prompted explicit discussion of its
counterexample in every review response; those responses kept the manager's
reported executions attributed and independently unverified here.

## Inputs and construction

`3d3107e8ee8e0ddb72f493d60864240772619c61` and matched the preparation corpus's
hashes. Twelve context files were taken from target
`0aaa6eafa06722add123105da912c906b8608633`. The full question guide stayed outside
recipient view; its exact instrument-discrimination section was supplied.
Each recipient could read its own payload and the same fifteen context files:
question, historical role files, recipe and eleven pinned source files.

The original payload has three unchanged documents. The common form places all
original bytes in 44 verbatim blocks under identity/completion/kind, answer,
support, qualifications and unresolved work. It tests organization of an existing
answer, not authoring against required typed fields. The omission removes 250
source lines across report and notes, including repeated versions of the central
argument. It also removes collateral material and leaves visible gaps. The
reordered arm reverses support-block order without changing any block or
specimen-internal event order. Payload sizes are 56,766 / 58,677 / 36,683 /
58,677 bytes respectively. The common form is not a compression experiment.

[Producer design](producer/attempt-01/DESIGN.md) and the private inventory at
`.scratch/answer-representation-01/producer/INVENTORY.json` give exact original
line spans, packet byte spans, omissions, identities and limits. Independent
original witnesses remain separate from the altered packets. The producer saw
historical decision metadata in the capture manifest and disclosed it; the
actual manager review was not read, and original assertions were preserved.

## Disposition and next work

This bounded commission completes the four-arm diagnostic pass and both reveals.
It establishes no durable schema, mandatory answer fields, custody policy or
permission to dispose of support. The accepted intellectual-agnosticism principle
is recorded in CONTRACTS and development guidance; it is the user's direction,
not a conclusion inferred from the pilot.

For a later comparison, keep direct expression, inspectable support, attribution
and reconstruction separate. Candidates worth testing are keeping evidence-class
qualifications adjacent to their claims and using a narrower omission that
preserves unrelated qualifications. These are proposals, not new obligations.
Repeat a consequential difference with fresh counterbalanced recipients before
using it for a design decision. A question-specific form, other question/return
types, and fresh authoring remain open under W01. Normal auditor-route trials
remain separately unqualified. No further study is dispatched in this window.

Keep the local `.scratch/answer-representation-01/` sources, packets, maps,
checks and frozen response copies with this result through comparison and review.
The explanatory records and recipient outputs live in ordinary project paths;
the local `.scratch` evidence population remains outside Git. The current
coordinator account records the user's recording commits. No pruning or removal
occurred.
