# Assessment recorded before recipient responses

This pilot examines representation effects, not the intellectual utility of the
audit. Historical-manager agreement is not an answer key. Source correctness
and attribution can be examined while substantive judgments remain those of
the recipients. Preserve differences and their reasons rather than averaging
them into a grade. One recipient per arm cannot establish a causal form effect.

Compare how each memo handles the original question and its support:

1. What claim can the recipient formulate, with which scope and qualifications?
2. Which support is cited directly from the payload, reconstructed from common
   historical context, requested as missing, or left unresolved?
3. Do predictions, reported historical observations, inspected source and current
   executions retain their distinct attribution? No execution is commissioned.
4. Does organization hide a condition, separate a qualification from its claim,
   or pressure a defect, remedy or definite result? Identify the passage and
   rival explanations rather than inferring pressure from a conclusion alone.
5. Does omitted support prompt an explicit limit/request, changed reasoning or
   additional reconstruction? Shared context, visible gaps and collateral cuts
   prevent describing the intervention as pure deprivation of one distinction.
6. Does reordering the same content change navigation, consulted sources or the
   substantive account? Such differences are leads about presentation and
   recipient variation, not a demand to restore a preferred conclusion.

The original return's recorder-origin inference stays present. Examine whether
the representation makes its supporting argument and qualifications available
for challenge, and whether the recipient repeats, contests or leaves it open.
Do not silently correct the original form or require agreement with later review.

After each initial memo is frozen, reveal the full original report and notes
with their provenance. A separate memo records what, if anything, changes and
why. Only after that memo is frozen may the historical manager review be shown,
as an attributed challenge whose claims are themselves examinable. A reveal
does not retroactively improve first-pass evidence. If time prevents a reveal,
record that stage as unrun rather than complete.

Record source/read-boundary compliance, packet digests, agent handles, launch
and return observations, timing/budget and missing artifacts. Procedural source
boundaries are not OS containment. A normal auditor call, fresh authoring and
actual custody/control qualification are separate evidence, not implied here.
