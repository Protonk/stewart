# Evidence needs before intake

1. Identify and authenticate the delivered result manifest against its external pin and the original commission receipt against its distinct external pin; determine what their hashes actually cover.
2. Read the original commission's exact question, context, constraints, and expected return sufficiently to distinguish commission recall from the auditor's later account.
3. Inspect the actual authored report and relevant support, retaining attribution for the auditor's observations, claims, and judgments.
4. Identify the audited C revision and the scope/conditions of any evidence offered for recall. Distinguish replayed assertions, independently checkable bytes, demonstrated reader behavior, and inaccessible historical execution.
5. Test only the supplied reader's permitted inspect/read operations when needed to retrieve or interpret preserved bytes. A reader demonstration alone does not establish behavior of the historical audited C revision.
6. Determine what the finite package permits a later release decision-maker to conclude, and state missing or unsupported evidence without filling gaps from unavailable context.
7. Record encountered inspection friction and evaluate the smallest response to an actual need: reader preview, a new result producer-and-reader slice, finite transition aid, or no extra feature. No need is predetermined.

Independent controls: preserve the external pins separately in this checkpoint; retain exact inspection outputs and hashes; distinguish container/member identity from semantic completeness and historical execution.
