# Predispatch assessment, coordinator/reviewer only

The consumer claim spans different kinds of binding. ACCEPTED contains the raw
byte digest/length and caller-supplied question/source/author/phase labels, plus
an explicit no-intellectual-assessment statement. SNAPSHOT carries the decoded
envelope, its selected texts and observations; payload.raw permits exact raw
identity and decoding comparison. The candidate producer source shows what the
helper would validate/write. Its historical execution identity and actual human/
agent conduct are not established merely by the receipt's labels. These are
design hypotheses for independent inspection, not a required answer supplied to
participants. No secret fact or preferred conclusion is the success criterion.

Observe whether the author distinguishes its argument from original selected
evidence, retains a qualified repeatable claim and minimum addition without
overpromising, and selects what its actual downstream use needs. The deliberate
base-context boundary makes authored delivery consequential. A self-contained
summary may still rely on undelivered original bytes; whether that matters must
be assessed against the exact claim, not word count or format compliance.

For reductions, inspect the actual selected bytes and redundancy before naming
the missing dependency. Prefer removing exact raw payload support while keeping
the author's analysis and any decoded companion, but remove only a reviewed
whole-artifact group. A companion may permit reconstruction; allow readers to
show that and distinguish reconstructed bytes from ordinary retrieval. If the
claimed raw identity can still be independently established, do not label it a
failed return just because the intended manipulation did not obstruct it.

Record separately: author-selected kind/completion where present; substantive
partial/reframed/narrowed claims; transport admission; recipient judgment; root
interpretation. Minimal form receives no invented kind. Unexpected findings must
be actual author-reported occurrences supported by process records, not our
planned omission or a change demanded by the prompt. False certainty, a supported
objection and a useful limited answer remain available outcomes.

The final brief should make a provisional recommendation, not add permanent
fields by habit. Test the case for external question/source/selection identity;
free text with ordered exact selected artifacts; optional semantic hints; explicit
shared context and uncertainty; new selection identities for corrections or
unchanged reselection. Keep custody-start, partial-attempt retention, durability,
recovery, enforcement and disposal decisions separate. A recommendation may be
qualified or unsupported where studies provide no distinguishing evidence.
