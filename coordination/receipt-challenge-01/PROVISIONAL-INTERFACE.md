# Provisional answer-interface recommendation

Independently reviewed coordinator recommendation. This is a recommendation under
the current commission, not a change to CONTRACTS, a frozen wire schema or authority
to implement a successor write path. It integrates the completed author/recipient
evidence from this receipt challenge with the earlier completed studies. The
[review](review/attempt-01/INTEGRATION-REVIEW.md) and
[disposition](REVIEW-DISPOSITION.md) preserve its qualifications and limits.

## Recommended common boundary

Use **a readable answer with ordered, explicitly selected artifacts**, tied by
the receiving operation to an identified commission and selection. Preserve the
exact submitted bytes, decoded content associations and attribution. Make semantic
outcome labels optional; require the content that the particular question needs
through its commission/recipe, without promoting that requirement into a universal
machine verdict on the answer.

The recommendation is deliberately provisional. It expresses the least common
structure supported by the observed cases and the decided intellectual-agnosticism
principle. It does not prove a minimal sufficient schema, field uselessness,
format equivalence or an optimal interaction model. A later concrete transport
may add mechanical fields for independently justified identity/custody purposes.

| Layer | Recommended content and reason | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Issued context | Explicit reference to the original question, selected source/context and stated reader use. Preserve the reader's authorized route to shared evidence. | A source label alone does not prove the source was consulted, faithfully executed or fully investigated. |
| Selection identity | A distinct record identity and attributable writer/submitter labels, bound to exact selected content and its commission. Identical payload bytes may be selected at different checkpoints. | Digest equality cannot substitute for selection identity; a caller label is not authenticated authorship. |
| Primary answer | Free text that can state a finding, qualified narrower claim, uncertainty, missing evidence, incomplete work, objection or proposed reframe, with its grounds and limits. | Presence or an `answer` label is not intellectual adequacy, truth, completion or manager acceptance. |
| Selected artifacts | Ordered logical names and exact content, with unambiguous references from the argument. Preserve association, bytes and evidence/interpretation attribution. | A path or digest alone does not deliver the corresponding bytes. A logical name never grants access or chooses a write path. |
| Optional semantic hints | If a consumer needs kind/completion/role hints, keep them attributed and subordinate to the actual argument; permit mixed or qualified scope. | No tested case establishes that the three prototype kinds exhaust permitted outcomes or that every artifact needs a typed role. |
| Capture account | Distinguish received raw input, structural admission/refusal, selected-byte observations and any recipient judgment. Preserve the scope and time of process claims. | Structural admission, a preserved receipt or a code listing is not evidence that the named investigation was performed. |

Do not require authors to duplicate machine-issued question/source/selection
identity merely to make it available to readers. If an author supplies an
identity claim, preserve it separately and expose disagreements instead of
silently replacing either value. The delivery must still bind the issued
identity explicitly; removing author duplication does not remove that duty.

The shared-context boundary is part of the undertaking. A reader should be able
to tell which material is carried in the return, which identified material is
explicitly available through the agreed context, and which premise remains
unsupported. A private working-directory citation cannot silently fill the gap.
Exact copying is one demonstrated delivery method; these studies do not require
every future artifact to be embedded UTF-8 text or rule out verified external
assets under a separately qualified custody mechanism.

## Question-specific expectations belong with the question

The unchanged bundled recipes supply these expectations. They are instructions
for producing and examining a response, not a proposal for Stewart to grade its
scientific merit or enforce a preferred outcome through admission.

| Question type | What the selected account should let its named reader inspect | Evidence presently informing the recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instrument discrimination | The comparison actually made; selected population and operands; contrasts changing the property and varying an irrelevant feature; assumptions and evidence ceiling. | [A3 representation](../answer-representation-01/RESULT.md) and [finite return](../return-boundary-01/RESULT.md) are retrospective. They distinguish preserved reasoning from reader reconstruction, without establishing general order invariance. |
| Information preservation | Actual producer/consumer sites and permitted companion data; a required distinction and meaningful pair; preservation, first supported loss/promotion or unresolved premise; consequence for the named use. | [Answer/revision](../answer-revision-01/RESULT.md) supplies fresh partial work, source-grounded completion, a self-review correction and identical reselection across two forms. |
| Constrained experiment construction | Exact construction and inspectable artifacts; hypotheses, predictions and limits; proposed versus executed commands. Where execution is granted, retain actual trial and preservation observations separately. | [Fresh construction](../fresh-authoring-01/RESULT.md) exposed an omitted observer dependency; [one bounded execution](../construction-execution-01/RESULT.md) later compared its frozen predictions with captured observations. |
| Receipt sufficiency | Premises of the named consumer claim; digest/reference/assertion/absent bindings; the weakest relied-on link, admitted substitution, minimum addition and exact qualified repeatable claim. | [This fresh challenge](RESULT.md) supplies two original selections whose central byte claims readers check, and two structurally admitted reductions whose missing operands readers distinguish. Historical conduct remains separately unbound. Preflight's exact snapshot-to-raw reconstruction shows why a filename omission alone is insufficient. |

A missing-evidence or reframed response must remain able to carry useful partial
reasoning and a precise next question. In the answer/revision study, common-form
a01 used prose/support to describe mixed scope beyond its discriminator; minimal
text also conveyed such distinctions without that field. The discriminator in
isolation was not tested, and these authors supplied different arguments.
This supports optional hints as the current recommendation, not a general finding
that labels mislead or never help navigation.

## What the studies do and do not support

The retrospective omission studies show why conclusion agreement is inadequate:
readers may reconstruct an argument, retain a similar conclusion and still lack
the author's delivered grounds or attribution. The fresh construction study
shows an actual selected artifact can be necessary to inspect a proposed command.
The answer/revision study shows both forms can carry conditional work and later
qualified accounts; it also preserves a real author correction and a reader's
acknowledged initial miss. Those observations support an inspectable content
boundary rather than a quality verdict inferred from schema success.

The receipt challenge adds a concrete delivered-support obstruction: both reduced
readers can inspect recorded labels and candidate source but cannot independently
repeat the retained raw/snapshot comparison claims. Both originals permit those
comparisons. Removing both companions also removes the complete decoded historical
response; this does not isolate serialization availability. Substantial useful
analysis remains, so the inadequacy concerns a particular claimed inspection.
The reduction is disclosed coordinator work, not an author failure or product loss.

Original delivery is not universal evidentiary completeness: r01 independently
identifies an ancillary manifest-check assertion whose operand a01 did not select.
Recipient judgment also remains contestable: r02's categorical snapshot-only
insufficiency statement is too broad given preflight's actual exact reconstruction.
Final review demonstrates the reverse direction from raw plus metadata/layout,
qualifying r04's categorical claim about recreated snapshot bytes. Both memos stay
unchanged, with these qualifications separate. A needed information binding need
not require one unique delivered filename if an attributed reconstruction can
be checked against the required identity. Original delivery, reconstruction and
historical execution nevertheless remain different claims. Neither conclusion
agreement nor a positive check count substitutes for inspecting those specific
claims. No field in either form automatically detects or resolves these issues.

The studies use few authors/readers, selected finite tasks, shared ambient
guidance and procedural grants. They do not establish population effects,
reading-time savings, optimal artifact counts, product size limits or human-user
usability. Question-specific rigid schemas have not been compared prospectively.
Ordinary unexpected findings, substantive reframing, refusal/blocked returns and
broader fresh instrument-discrimination work retain their actual coverage status.
Do not keep running similar affirmative cases merely to accumulate agreement;
any next empirical work should name the design choice and rival it distinguishes.

## Decisions still required before a durable write path

This recommendation concerns selected content. It does not choose whether
Stewart's custody starts with the first partial attempt or only an admitted final
selection, nor whether setters or one complete submission are the supported
interaction. Rejected attempts, interrupted writes, partial work, correction and
finality need an explicit W02 policy. Raw preservation before interpretation
remains a decided process obligation; these experiments do not discharge or
silently weaken it.

Likewise, a finite readable return does not authorize disposal of unselected work.
Existing preservation undertakings, hold/live-use protections and interrupted
custody operations require their supported disposition. No new audit execution,
restart facility, automatic retirement or release/run stage follows from a
selection or correction identity. Product access, stopping, delivery/retrieval,
durability and recovery still need their own design and real operating evidence.

The next decision discussion should evaluate this common content boundary and
explicit unsupported capabilities together with custody-start/finality choices.
Keep unresolved operational commitments visible in that connected design; do
not treat this recommendation as readiness to ship the successor write path.
