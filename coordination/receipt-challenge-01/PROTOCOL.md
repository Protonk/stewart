# Fixed comparison and stopping rules

Two fresh authors receive identical full subject packets. a01 uses the existing
common envelope; a02 uses minimal text/artifacts. No model override or shared
conversation history. Each selects one frozen return plus a private process
account and actual read/effect ledger. No imposed kind or later source release.
One author per form is exploratory; variability, wording and artifact organization
remain rivals, so no causal format superiority/equivalence is claimed.

The subject comprises exact retained ACCEPTED.json, SNAPSHOT.json and payload.raw
from answer-revision a03 phase 02, plus the exact candidate capture.py bytes and
the unchanged recipe. The new consumer citation is separately issued. Initial
copies are verified against the prior completion inventory and pinned Git source.
No separate earlier study interpretation or private process account is in either
grant. The historical answer embedded in the raw/snapshot subject is necessarily
present as the artifact under inspection, not as a receipt-sufficiency answer key.

Author output: SELECTED.json, PROCESS.md, READS.json, PROGRESS.md in exclusive
attempt directories. The question says precisely what the downstream reader
shares and which original companions must be selected if relied upon. Original
companions use exact text in separate objects; the analysis organization is free.
This is an imposed delivery rule and limits generalization to unrestricted layouts.

Root freezes every selected raw file and private account before invoking the
unchanged reviewed helper in a fresh destination. Common/minimal schemas retain
the same 1 MiB / 32-object limits. Raw witness, command, PID, streams, exit and
admitted snapshot remain. A rejection is preserved; one mechanical correction
may use a new filename/capture, with content changes attributed. No silent repair,
overwriting, directory reuse or automatic rerun after uncertain effects.

## Controlled recipient variants

Four fresh recipients: r01 sees a01 original; r02 sees a01 reduced variant;
r03 sees a02 original; r04 sees a02 reduced variant. They get only the base subject
context promised in QUESTION plus selected content and external capture/variant
provenance. All are told whether theirs is original or coordinator-reduced; no
coordinator edits are attributed to the author. They do not read private author
accounts, other arms, original full packets or historical study interpretations.
There is one reader phase. No recipient reveal is planned.

Reduction rule, fixed before authors: remove the smallest **whole selected
artifact dependency group** whose absence prevents independently checking a
specific material claim retained in the unchanged primary/remaining content.
Prefer the exact payload.raw companion and any selected redundant exact copy
which would otherwise supply those bytes. Keep original metadata, primary and
all retained objects/text/order unchanged. Inspect redundancies: a filename's
absence alone does not establish a support gap. SNAPSHOT may preserve decoded
content without establishing the original raw serialization. Record precisely
which distinction becomes unavailable and what stays inspectable. If a verified
reconstruction supplies a missing binding, attribute it and retain that result;
do not force failure. No corrupting historical evidence or concealing edits.

The reviewer must approve a concrete reduction and its support-dependence before
reader dispatch. If neither return supports a genuine whole-artifact challenge,
retain that as an unexercised case and explain the obstruction in the decision
brief; do not manufacture author content, force an outcome or silently switch
to arbitrary prose deletion. Variant serialization is coordinator work, captured
separately with explicit provenance and structurally admitted by the same helper.
Structural success is not a judgment of the retained intellectual claim.

The original provides a complete-content comparison. The reduction is the
sensitivity case; unchanged primary, retained artifacts and base context provide
byte invariances. Readers perform the named manager task, locate exact grounds/
gaps, distinguish missing authored delivery from unavailable historical evidence
and outside requests, and attribute their own reconstruction. One reader per
arm cannot establish an agent-independent detection rate or reading-time benefit.

## Integration and qualification limits

Independent review checks design before authors, variants before recipients,
and all originals/process/read accounts, variants, recipient memos and root's
decision brief before completion. Review uses frozen snapshots and actual counts;
mutable progress is not retrospectively compared as immutable evidence.

Root records content requirements, obstruction/qualification and open cases,
then proposes a provisional common interface and per-question expectations using
this and earlier completed studies. Distinguish recommendations from decided
contracts, information needed from fields made compulsory, and legitimate partial/
reframed work from transport failure. Retain contradictory assessments. No
preferred substantive conclusion or agreement score measures success.

No new subject, installed-wheel or pytest execution is planned. Prior helper
qualification remains a separate historical run. Only this study's capture
invocations and static checks count here. Missing evidence/reconstruction cannot
be presented as ordinary delivered support. All finite witnesses, originals,
failures and variants remain under .scratch/receipt-challenge-01 with RESULT.md.
No cleanup, product custody, access/control/delivery or disposal qualification.
