Without a faithful-recording assumption, the earliest unsupported transition is from a record saying acceptance occurred to the occurrence of that historical event. The inspected receipt has no observed-input binding to distinguish that case. Authorship, completed investigation and the executed-writer premise remain separate gaps even if one is assumed.
