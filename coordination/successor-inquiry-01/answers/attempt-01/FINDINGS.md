### Assessment and controls

Assess task completion, retained qualifications, inspectable support, exact
repeatable claim, unsupported inference and recipient/author effort separately
from schema validity. For each requirement record supported, correct but
unsupported/incomplete, missing, or incorrect; do not average away serious loss.
Check specifically that:

* `unmet` gates interpretation; zero summary/empty anomalies/exit 0/control
  firing alone do not. The release review owns membership and denominator.
* P-sub differs from O-op; K-swap does not prove strict-mode firing from an
  aggregate result; accepted row counts retain their meaning.
* Predictions remain predictions; the unsupported recorder-origin inference
  is challenged or explicitly left for verification, not promoted into fact.
* Fail-closed/negative findings, optional follow-ups and assumptions remain
  distinct and useful.

Two controls accompany M:

* **M-minus, firing omission:** keep the envelope valid but remove support for
  reviewed membership and the P-sub/O-op distinction, including redundant
  equivalents. Preserve the omission map outside the recipient view. Assessment
  must detect lost support, an evidence request or extra reconstruction effort.
  Calling it equally useful merely because it is valid fails the assessment.
  No invented result is inserted.
* **M-order, invariance:** reorder/format the same labeled evidence blocks,
  preserving operands and qualifications. Never reorder events inside a
  specimen. Scientific decision should remain invariant; navigation costs are
  a separate result. A difference challenges a claimed content effect.

The smallest diagnostic pass is O/M/M-minus/M-order with fresh separate
