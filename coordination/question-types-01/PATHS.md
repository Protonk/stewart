# Three paths for questions crossing Stewart

Discussion proposals, 2026-10-29. The user asked for three distinct approaches
without a preference signal or classification by degree of change. None is adopted.
The manager custody/material contracts and auditor retry/re-entry agreement stand;
this discussion concerns question shapes, material expectations and answer forms.
No runtime implementation, model consultation or experiment was performed.

The user values Stewart having opinions about question/material/evidence pairings
and teaching manager agents through tool shape and replies. The constraint is
usable commissioning: broad requests must have a way through without a sequence
of corrective demands that defeats the manager's task. A residual type may be a
normal part of the instrument, not a label for inadmissible or inferior questions.

## Common ground for the comparison

Use the same example: "Audit our app to make sure there are no bugs," with source
files and some existing test reports. All three approaches can commission this
request after ordinary mechanical preparation. They preserve the manager's words,
use only the captured material and current read-only tools, and keep the usual
custody, return and close behavior. An auditor can inspect supplied reports and
reason about code, but these paths do not add execution, outside research or
additional agents. A hypothetical failure path is not an actually executed test.

Opinions are visible in versioned instructions and expected response structure.
Missing evidence, disagreement with the framing, no finding and a supported
bounded assurance all remain expressible. Neither a form selection nor Stewart's
validation certifies the app bug-free. It preserves the auditor's claim and basis.
No substantive conclusion has to be produced to satisfy a mandatory finding slot.

Preparation can show a short relevant material-role observation or a suggested
shape, but does not demand repeated acknowledgment of a broad question. An
unassigned material role is not proof the required evidence is absent from every
attached file; Stewart distinguishes declared mappings from auditor judgments.
Normal mechanical refusals still apply to invalid handles, nonexistent referenced
items, unsupported input bytes and the established intake limits.

## A. An open inquiry with evidence modules

Every commission begins as an open question. It has a common answer convention:
state what was examined, what the evidence supports and what remains unresolved.
The manager may add named modules such as comparison, failure path or claim
support. Each adds material-role prompts and a corresponding way to record
evidence, without replacing the question or requiring selection of one exclusive
question category. Module choices are explicit, not secretly inferred from prose.

For comparison, the prompts identify the alternatives and criterion. A failure
path prompts for the entry point, expected behavior and source or supplied trace
supporting the alleged deviation. The auditor can distinguish a supplied
expectation from an inferred one, or state that it cannot establish one. A claim
module asks for supporting and challenging evidence without requiring a defect
or a repair. A missing role produces a short preparation observation; it does
not force an invented attachment or a fresh round of justification.

For the broad bug request, the manager can commission the open inquiry directly,
or add a failure-path module. The original request stays verbatim. The latter
asks the auditor to connect any suspected defect to a concrete behavior and its
basis, rather than merely list plausible worries. Findings remain bounded by what
was actually examined; a report of no finding has an ordinary completion path.

**Residual route:** an open inquiry with no modules. It receives the same
custody, investigative means and ability to submit; it is not a degraded tier.

**What this buys:** task-specific evidence expectations can be attached to a
question that spans categories, and the manager can add only useful structure.
**What it costs:** modules can overlap or impose competing demands. Choosing
none bypasses their particular opinions; choosing several can burden the auditor
with more structure than the task needs. Their interactions require evaluation.

## B. The auditor frames the investigable question

The manager commissions an inquiry into the stated concern. Part of the auditor's
job is to identify the precise question it can responsibly investigate using the
attached materials, then do that work within the same commission. The answer
records the original request, the auditor's working question, its relationship to
the request, the evidence examined and the answer it supports. A well-specified
original question can simply be retained as the working question.

Stewart's instructions have opinions about useful working questions: identify
the behavior or proposition, state what would bear on it, and distinguish missing
material from a negative result. The auditor's framing is attributed to the
auditor, never substituted into the accepted manager question. Narrowing does not
grant new material, tools, budget, another run or a second investigation.

For the bug request, the auditor might investigate whether the supplied retry
logic permits charging an order twice. It reports the code path and supporting
or contrary material it actually examined, and explains the portion of the broad
request left unanswered. This is substantive inspection during the original
commission, not a mandatory first audit that returns only a better question.
When the material cannot support even a useful bounded investigation, an account
of that limitation and the question/material needed is itself a permitted return.

**Residual route:** inquiry whose framing is part of the work. Broadness or a
poor match to existing question categories is a reason to use it, not to refuse it.

**What this buys:** a manager can bring an unresolved concern without first
learning how to phrase a specialized audit. Returns teach through concrete
examples of question/evidence pairings and include actual investigative work.
**What it costs:** the auditor gains discretion over framing. It may choose a
tractable issue that misses the manager's real concern. There is no new mid-run
approval channel: that choice and its limits must be visible in the returned work.

## C. A library of question families

Stewart offers a small set of named families, such as comparison, behavior check,
claim examination and open inquiry. Each family supplies a preparation scaffold,
material-role prompts, auditor instructions and expected answer structure.
The manager chooses the family; a suggestion cannot silently select another one
or rewrite the question. The library is where specific opinions are authored,
versioned, inspected and revised.

A comparison identifies the things compared and the criterion. A behavior check
distinguishes expected behavior, supplied observations and the implementation.
A claim examination invites support, counterevidence and qualifications; it does
not require a first defective premise or an addition that repairs the claim.
Unassigned material roles are visible but do not block commissioning. The auditor
may contest the premise or explain that the family is a poor fit.

For the bug request, Stewart can offer a behavior-check scaffold whose question
is "Where does the supplied implementation appear to violate a stated or inferred
expectation?" The manager may use that wording or keep the original. The family
asks the auditor to state the expectation, evidential basis and qualifications
of each claimed deviation. A manager wanting a broader inquiry can select open
and commission the original request without defending that choice.

**Residual route:** open inquiry alongside the named families, with common
expectations for an evidence-based account and its limits. It has no compulsory
explanation for avoiding the other families, repeated warning or reduced service.

**What this buys:** a known kind of task gets a complete question/material/answer
pairing, and similar commissions can return similarly organized evidence.
**What it costs:** the manager must recognize a useful family. An attractive but
ill-fitting scaffold can distort the question; managers may routinely choose open
instead. The library needs restraint to avoid becoming an expanding taxonomy.

## What is not selected by this briefing

No path, mixture of paths, concrete schema, mandatory slot, citation refusal,
substantive stopping rule, hidden prior or rendered-answer packaging is adopted.
After the question approach is chosen, specify these as one coherent instrument
and test what its participants can express, including disagreement with the form.
Filling fields or agreeing with a preferred conclusion is not evidence of a good
instrument. Relevant comparisons include manager effort, abandoned/rephrased
requests, invented evidence to satisfy a prompt, unacknowledged scope changes and
whether readers can distinguish a limited finding from a broad assurance claim.
