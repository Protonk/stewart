# This folder is the record of a closed design conversation

`linux-mvp-01/` records one design conversation between the user and two
coordinators on 2026-10-28, and the documents it produced. The conversation
ended at turn 23. It is a record, not guidance.

- **CONTRACTS.md owns the decisions.** The user's decisions from the conversation
  are recorded there (the Mission and "Successor interfaces decided 2026-10-28").
  Nothing in this folder decides anything on its own.
- **The conversation is closed.** `CONVERSATION.md` and
  `CONVERSATION-HELD-BACK.md` hold the user's words verbatim and the coordinators'
  replies as given. Do not edit them and do not add turns. New design discussion
  goes to CONTRACTS and the active plan, not here.
- **`ROUTER.md` and `END-TO-END.md` are settled as of turn 23.** They change only
  when CONTRACTS changes, at the user's direction, with the change noted at the
  top. Where they and CONTRACTS differ, CONTRACTS decides.
- **Earlier documents are records of the path.** `DESIGN.md`, `STEWART.md` and
  `RESERVATIONS.md` show how the design got where it is. Leave them as they are.
- **Attribution matters here.** The user's directions are the quoted prompts in
  the conversation. Much of the design's detail is coordinator proposal.
  `RESERVATIONS.md` has a table separating the two for the first design, and
  CONTRACTS' decided section separates them for the rest. When citing this
  folder, say which kind of statement you are relying on.

[README.md](README.md) is the reading guide.
