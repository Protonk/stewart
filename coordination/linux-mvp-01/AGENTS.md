# This folder is a record of a design conversation

`linux-mvp-01/` records one design conversation between the user and the
coordinator on 2026-10-28, and the documents it produced. It is a record and a
working proposal. It is not guidance, and it is not a decision.

- **Nothing here changes Stewart's decided behavior.** CONTRACTS.md owns that. The
  router and its end-to-end tests become decided only when the user adopts them and
  CONTRACTS records it.
- **Treat the conversation files as captured history.** `CONVERSATION.md` and
  `CONVERSATION-HELD-BACK.md` hold the user's words verbatim and the coordinator's
  replies as given. Don't edit them. Add later turns at the end of
  `CONVERSATION.md` if the conversation continues here.
- **Earlier documents are records of the path.** `DESIGN.md`, `STEWART.md` and
  `RESERVATIONS.md` show how the design got where it is. Leave them as they are.
- **`ROUTER.md` and `END-TO-END.md` are the latest working statements.** Change them
  only at the user's direction, and record the turn that asked for the change.
- **Attribution matters here.** The user's directions are quoted in the
  conversation. Much of the design's detail is the coordinator's proposal.
  `RESERVATIONS.md` has a table separating the two for the first design, and the
  same distinction applies to everything after it. When citing this folder, say
  which kind of statement you are relying on.

[README.md](README.md) is the reading guide.
