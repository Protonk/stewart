## The job custody serves

I understand the mission as enabling someone to commission a bounded inquiry,
give the auditor room to investigate, receive an attributable and inspectable
return, and finish without continually supervising the work or reconstructing
what happened. Custody makes the material handoffs dependable. Intellectual
judgment remains with the participants: faithful custody can preserve a weak
answer, a reframe or an account of missing evidence.

Custody is more than finding a file on disk. Stewart accepts responsibility for
identified bytes and their relevant context, for access through the promised
interface, for accounting for its partial effects, and for disposing only within
its authority. Different material has different custodians and lifetimes.

## What that means for the decided successor direction

1. **The original undertaking.** The question, instructions, selected input copies,
   relevant guidance and declared grants/limits establish what was commissioned.
   Receipts bind identities and accepted selections to recoverable bytes. This
   supports recall and comparison despite later edits or lost conversational
   context. C already delivers its independent bundle into manager custody; it
   supplies no private indefinite retention service. The later audit service must
   explicitly take responsibility for the finite context its result promises to
   carry. Its exact acquisition point and binding are preparation work, not yet
   implemented continuity between two services.
2. **Recorded answer material during authoring.** Successful add holds exact bytes
   and an element identity beyond the helper's life. Replace/delete change current
   membership; superseded payloads stay internally held until authorized close.
   That simplifies safe editing without a draft promotion or history-browser
   obligation. Submit or manager collect fixes one selection and its attribution.
   Its intellectual completeness is a separate judgment.
3. **The account needed to honor those undertakings.** Operation identities,
   manifest bindings, outcomes, failure diagnostics and disposal authority let
   Stewart distinguish an unfinished effect from a completed effect with a lost
   reply. This lets participants continue through ordinary interfaces without
   guessing or private-state repair. It does not automatically require preserving
   every tool call, conversation, execution environment or historical dependency.
4. **Audit-owned working facilities and their material.** Stewart supplies and
   protects facilities for the bounded investigation and is responsible for their
   authorized retirement. That creates obligations beyond the selected answer:
   partial work, active dependencies and promised preservation cannot be disposed
   of just because a manifest is complete. A finite, explicitly recorded hold
   constrains removal; it adds no investigation or live-access authority. The
   manager's independent workspaces remain outside successor disposal scope.
5. **The fixed handback until its undertaking is finished.** Stewart owes an
   independent manager copy of the original question/instructions, commission
   receipt, sealed manifest, selected bytes and provenance, with honest gaps.
   Copy completion, manager receipt and the manager's safety judgment remain
   distinct. Affirmative ordinary close ends audit-held copying; the delivered
   copy remains under manager preservation. Other named obligations and holds do
   not disappear merely because that selected package arrived.

The finite return need not contain every original input or every referenced
object. Original input custody through C and later answer-package custody have
distinct responsibilities. That separation must stay intelligible to a manager
who wants to inspect the basis of an answer; it promises no complete audit replay.

## What exists in executable form today

Frozen Python performs the actual audit lifecycle: retained commission/Git
identities, an audit checkout and supplied runtime, immutable custody records,
captured reports and changed/support material, retained/exported products and
gated close. Its broader execution/resource machinery is historical implementation,
not a requirement to port. C implements the bounded original-input bundle and
readback with manager-owned preservation. The separate authoring prototype checks
part of the edit/finality mechanism. It is not the installed successor audit
service. The complete simplified relationship above remains partly decided work.

## Where I am uncomfortable

The main unresolved boundary is **workspace material never added to the answer**.
Suppose the auditor writes a useful analysis.csv in its working area and adds
only summary.md. Collect can correctly deliver every recorded member and still
leave analysis.csv outside that return. A complete manifest cannot by itself
authorize deleting that file. Conversely, calling every working byte permanent
evidence would broaden custody without a finite need. Existing contracts preserve
partial-work obligations and allow explicitly declared disposable categories;
the exact successor allocation has not been settled. I would examine this boundary
before implementing environment removal, keeping the authoring simplification
intact. This is not permission to add a draft lifecycle or silently classify all
unregistered work as disposable.

I am also uncomfortable with **uncertainty becoming permanent custody by default**.
Keeping damaged material while its fate is unresolved is an honest interim state.
It is not a complete account of how a bounded inquiry ends. Unknown selection and
unknown removal ownership are different problems: inability to enumerate an
answer does not itself establish that a directory belongs to Stewart, or that it
does not. Any later disposition must state the uncertainty and still respect
verified removal authority and holds. This is a concern for discussion, not new
unknown-inventory closing authority. The proposed no-removal implementation leg
can proceed with an explicit unfinished obligation; a complete product eventually
needs a supported conclusion.

Retaining superseded bytes until close feels like a reasonable temporary space
tradeoff. The check is whether lifetime and purpose stay finite, especially with
holds or unfinished closure. Likewise duplication between a manager's C bundle,
audit context and delivered result buys independence only if each copy has an
explicit owner and promise. The audit already identified that concrete binding
work. These are reasons to keep obligations narrow and legible, not reasons to
weaken successful-add or durable-delivery promises.

The prior audit assessed internal coherence of a bounded proposed leg. This
shakedown asks whether the whole custody arrangement remains proportionate to
the mission. The two questions can have different useful answers. No new audit,
implementation or turnover is commissioned by this account.
