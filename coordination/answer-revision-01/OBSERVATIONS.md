## Bounded source result

Under the issued session identities, collected HEAD, hypothetical report bytes
and stipulated matching-digest reads, the entire returned dictionary retains
the four required states. `status.py:92–94` passes the validator result into
both claim entries. Nonvalid outer values are null, but nested validation still
distinguishes missing, invalid and mismatch; a mismatch retains its declaration,
expected/observed operands and diagnostics. The entries attribute completion and
report kind to the auditor and disclaim process-exit or manager-judgment meaning.
This is the participants' source-supported account, corroborated by root's static
inspection. Validity is not truth, authenticity, completed work or endorsement.

The valid/mismatch pair changes only target_commit, with both spellings accepted
by the grammar; it isolates identity comparison. Missing versus duplicate-key
invalid keeps outer values null but changes status/diagnostics. The prose variant
holds declaration bytes fixed and predicts an unchanged full returned dictionary
under the same session/name inputs. These are static contrasts and predictions,
not executed behavioral controls or proof about arbitrary prose/fence changes.

Early branches bound the claim. Missing session and unavailable/mismatched reads
produce reasons without validation. Missing expected digest validates synthetic
empty bytes and clears source, so its missing status does not describe a report
that was read. `evidence_tampered` combines unavailability and digest mismatch;
it does not establish intent or distinguish those causes. With absent collected
HEAD that identity comparison is omitted. These limits are inspectable alongside
the qualified finding; no repair follows for the commissioned readable-input use.

## Requirements, obstructions and open cases

These are implications for provisional interface discussion, not adopted product
contracts or compulsory-field decisions.

| Requirement or obstruction illustrated | Direct support | Boundary of the inference |

case rather than repeat this affirmative static route merely to accumulate agreement.
