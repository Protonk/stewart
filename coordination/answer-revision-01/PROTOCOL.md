## Subject and information boundary

Use exact source bytes from HEAD 5f00082. Extracted source ranges carry their
original path, revision, line ranges and whole-file digest; no edits or synthetic
replacement functions. Initial common material supplies declaration validation,
parser dependencies and hypothetical report bytes/identity inputs. The bounded
consumer is status._auditor_report; the complete arms have its exact definition,
the delayed arms are explicitly told its implementation is absent. No other
file in a delayed grant may reveal its implementation. Omitted source, full
witnesses and assessment stay outside participant grants. Source is not secrecy-
enforced in this shared repository; read ledgers and grant compliance are observed
accounts, not proof of complete process access monitoring.

The named use requires distinguishing missing, invalid, identity-mismatched and
valid declarations and retaining the difference between an auditor claim and an
observed process exit or manager judgment. Consumer projection is allowed when
the required distinction remains available with its attribution. Raw report
prose is not a completion declaration. The question asks where the supplied
route preserves, loses, promotes or leaves a required distinction unresolved.
It does not prescribe a finding. Safe capture/read enforcement, higher-level
collection selection, rendering, close and all unrelated routes are excluded.
Treat the supplied session identities and read results as stipulated entry inputs,
not qualified filesystem or receipt authenticity. No actual custody record is
created. Hypothetical fixture bytes are labeled as such.

## Answer forms and captures

Common form retains the prior prototype's fields: question_id, source_revision,
author, response_kind (answer / needs-evidence / reframe), completion_claim,
answer, and support objects with id/role/encoding/text. Partial and mixed work can
be expressed in free text; a single response_kind is a form constraint to observe,
not evidence that the work has only one kind. Do not force any field value.

Minimal form has text plus an ordered artifact array of name/text objects.
Question/source/author/phase identity comes from the issued assignment and capture
record; no author-supplied response kind, completion field or artifact role is
required. Both carry UTF-8 text bytes via JSON transport, with the same generous
1 MiB raw bound and 32 selected artifacts; no truncation, dereferencing or directory
sweep. Capture preserves raw bytes before validation, exact decoded bytes/order
and hashes, using fresh destinations. The implementation and its meaningful
controls are reviewed before author dispatch. Neither structural admission nor
schema rejection is an intellectual verdict or product custody claim.

Authors save SELECTED-01.json and later SELECTED-02.json in their owned attempt;
root freezes each before invoking transport. A rejected selection and all helper
outputs remain. One mechanical correction per phase is permitted in a new named
file/attempt, with any content change attributed; no silent normalization. If it
still cannot be admitted, retain the failure and give the recipient an explicit
unavailable-return account. Preserve usable raw content for later independent
review; any rescue is separately labeled and cannot count as ordinary receipt.
This is a study correction rule, not a proposed product retry facility.

## Observations and controls

Freeze assessment before dispatch. Record submitted bytes, objects, rejected
attempts, author-declared effort/friction, actual reads, substantive changes and
essential dependence on free text. No cap pressure is presumed. The gap arms are
a sensitivity opportunity; report whether the author/reader identifies the actual
missing dependency and whether the release changes the grounded account. Complete
arms receiving repeated source are controls for changes caused by a second look.
No enforced equality or change is required. Cross-format contrasts hold evidence
schedule fixed but each has one author/reader, so agent variation remains a rival.

Distinguish added source access, a new source-grounded finding, a correction,
reclassification, elaboration and mere repetition. A staged release cannot be
advertised as an unexpected discovery during ordinary uninterrupted investigation.
No forced formal needs-evidence/partial/reframe outcome; record which arise and
what remains unexercised. Disagreement stays attributed and inspectable.

Independent preflight reviews packet identity/membership, withheld-source absence,
question/recipe fidelity, transport controls and design limits. Final review checks
every frozen return, recipient memo, process account and evidence population,
and challenges integration claims. Root dispositions findings and records useful
requirements, obstruction/qualification and untested cases. Zero installed-wheel/
pytest nodes and zero subject executions are planned; standalone transport and
static byte checks are reported separately. Preserve all witnesses and failures
through review, with no removal or original historical receipt rewrite.
