# Broader auditor studies

Use bounded encounters to learn how Stewart supports an inquiry: discoverability,
material navigation, citation repair, recording/revision and explicit submission.
A useful answer with less mechanical effort is a success; minimizing token counts
or polishing one model's prose is not the objective. Question meaning and answer
quality remain matters of attributed judgment.

- [ ] Finish the comparison of answer sufficiency and interface friction.

  authority, expressive freedom or custody require the principal's decision.
