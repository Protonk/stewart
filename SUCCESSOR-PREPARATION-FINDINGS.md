## Next commissions and verification

The proposed next empirical commission is the A3 representation pilot. It needs
no Claude launch, private-store access, runtime changes or fresh product audit.
One producer would prepare a common form with a statement/omission source map;
an independent reviewer would verify its fidelity before recipients see it.
Four fresh recipients would then receive one arm each: original answer and
notes, common form, common form missing a necessary support distinction, or
the same common-form evidence in a different order. Save their initial decision
memos before revealing omitted support, and reveal the manager review last.

The recipient task is the original manager decision: determine when a
zero-disagreement statement is justified, which operands and qualifications
matter, and what follow-up is required. Schema validity is separate from
usefulness. The omission arm must expose missing support or reconstruction
effort; the reordered arm tests content invariance. Preserve the original
answer's false ancillary inference rather than silently correcting it.
A rescue after revealing support counts as a first-pass shortcoming.

Use fresh agents without inherited inquiry history, finite source packets and
explicit read boundaries; save consulted sources and reject contaminated
observations. This is a procedural recipient-information boundary, not a claim
of OS containment. With the current four-slot limit, producer/reviewer work
precedes recipients, which run in batches of at most three. Do not reuse one
recipient across arms. A single recipient per arm is diagnostic only; repeat
consequential differences with fresh counterbalanced recipients before relying
on them. A question-specific form follows the common-form result. This bounds
the first commission and leaves general schema acceptance to later evidence.

In parallel or afterward, a separately commissioned source-only task can make
the helper export-to-fresh-source route concrete. Actual legacy inventory needs
finite authorized stores and the worker's inventory brief; absence of access
must remain unknown, not zero. Route probes require named hosts, versions,
controllers and receiving endpoints. Any model-consuming Claude probe needs
the user's explicit permission and usable quota; the deliberate limit study
remains deferred. No route, prototype, inventory or representation pilot is
started by this preparation report.
